1. MODULE OVERVIEW & THEOLOGICAL PURPOSE
================================================================================
The AI Sermon Assistant is a dedicated theological copilot engineered to assist pastors, 
preachers, and ministry leaders in outlining, structuring, and drafting Bible-based 
sermons with exceptional speed and theological depth.

Key Principles:
* Scripture Centricity: Scripture citations remain uncompromised and distinguished from 
  AI commentary.
* Cultural & Linguistic Authenticity: Built from the ground up to support Telugu (`తెలుగు`) 
  ministry alongside English, Hindi, Tamil, and Kannada.
* Situational Relevance: Equips pastors for sudden pastoral duties (e.g., unexpected 
  funeral, hospital prayer, wedding blessing, thanksgiving meeting) with tailored 
  Scripture references and pastoral words.
* Reversible AI Refinement: Features one-tap AI modifier chips to instantly rewrite, 
  expand, or simplify sermon points without losing the original outline.

4. STEP 2: CONTEXT & CUSTOMIZATION CONTROLS
================================================================================
Fine-tunes the output to match the specific congregation and service length.

4.1 Additional Topic / Focus Input
----------------------------------
* TextInput field: "e.g. Love and Commitment" or "Walking in God's Unshakable Peace".
* Allows entering custom sermon themes, sermon series titles, or specific Scripture chapters.

4.2 Audience Targeting (8 Profiles)
-----------------------------------
Horizontal selection pills:
1. Congregation (General all-member Sunday worship service)
2. Youth (Engaging, practical, addressing modern challenges)
3. Children (Story-driven, simple vocabulary, memorable takeaways)
4. Families (Household harmony, marriage, parenting)
5. Men (Leadership, godly character, integrity)
6. Women (Faith, encouragement, virtuous lifestyle)
7. Seniors (Comfort, eternal hope, legacy of faith)
8. Leaders (Pastoral duty, servant leadership, ministry excellence)

4.3 Language Selection
----------------------
Supports 5 major Indian and global languages:
* English
* Telugu (తెలుగు)
* Hindi (हिन्दी)
* Tamil (தமிழ்)
* Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ)

4.4 Duration Configuration
--------------------------
Adjusts outline depth and point count:
* 10 min: Concise outline with 2 key points (ideal for cottage prayers).
* 20 min: Focused message with 3 points.
* 30 min: Standard Sunday morning sermon (3-4 points with illustrations).
* 45 min: Detailed expository preaching (4 points with theological commentary).
* 60 min: Comprehensive convention / seminar layout.

5. SERMON GENERATION ENGINE & PROGRESSIVE FEEDBACK
================================================================================
5.1 AI Generation Pipeline
--------------------------
* Tapping the gradient button "Generate Sermon":
  1. Compiles the full prompt payload including category, audience, length, and language.
  2. Dispatches to `AIService.generateSermon`.
  3. Sends request authenticated with the church's API credentials.
  4. Automatically stores generated outline into Firestore history.

5.2 5-Stage Animated Preparation Status Indicator
-------------------------------------------------
While waiting (approximately 10-12 seconds), the UI rotates through 5 informative 
pastoral status steps:
1. "Connecting to AI Sermon Assistant..."
2. "Searching Scriptures & Bible references..."
3. "Structuring sermon points & insights..."
4. "Adding pastoral applications & prayer..."
5. "Finalizing [Language] sermon..."

6. SERMON STRUCTURE & INTERACTIVE FORMATTING
================================================================================
Generated sermons are parsed into structured, distinct visual blocks:

6.1 Title Card & AI Badge
-------------------------
* High-contrast card with golden badge: `★ AI SERMON OUTLINE`.
* Formatted bold sermon title (e.g., "దేవుని నిరీక్షణలో నడుచుట - Walking in Divine Hope").

6.2 Section Header & Quick-Jump Navigation Bar
----------------------------------------------
A horizontal navigation bar pinned above the sermon text allowing one-tap scrolling 
directly to specific sections:
* [Introduction] (Indigo pill) ➔ Jumps to opening hook & background
* [Key Scriptures] (Purple pill) ➔ Jumps to primary Scripture readings
* [Main Message] (Violet pill) ➔ Jumps to sermon body & expository points
* [Takeaways] (Amber pill) ➔ Jumps to life application & practical steps
* [Closing Prayer] (Emerald pill) ➔ Jumps to benediction & altar call

6.3 Point-by-Point Expository Breakdown
---------------------------------------
* Each sermon point is rendered with a dedicated badge (e.g., `Point 1`, `Point 2`, `Point 3`).
* Includes bold key truth statement followed by contextual analysis.

6.4 Bible Reference Badges & Scripture Quotes
---------------------------------------------
* Purple badge card: `BIBLE BOOK & CHAPTER • వాక్య ఆధారం`.
* Highlights specific Bible references (e.g., "రోమీయులకు 8:28 / Romans 8:28").
* Scripture quotations are encased in reverent italic cards.

6.5 Practical Takeaways & Closing Altar Call Prayer
---------------------------------------------------
* Amber Card: `PRACTICAL TAKEAWAY • ఆచరణాత్మక సత్యం` with practical action steps.
* Emerald Card: Closing pastoral prayer and altar call invitation ready for the preacher 
  to lead the congregation in prayer.

7. REAL-TIME SERMON REFINEMENT CHIPS (AI MODIFIERS)
================================================================================
Located directly beneath the sermon display. Pastors can tap any modifier chip to 
instantly rewrite or adjust the sermon while keeping the same core theme:

1. "Regenerate" (RefreshCw icon):
   - Re-generates a completely new sermon structure from scratch.
2. "Expand Intro" (AlignLeft icon):
   - Instructs AI: "Please expand the introduction to be more detailed and engaging."
3. "More Verses" (BookMarked icon):
   - Instructs AI: "Please include more relevant Bible verses and scriptures throughout."
4. "Add Point" (ListPlus icon):
   - Instructs AI: "Please add one additional major point to the sermon outline."
5. "Simplify" (Minimize icon):
   - Instructs AI: "Please simplify the language so it is easier for a general audience."
6. "More Detail" (Maximize icon):
   - Instructs AI: "Please expand on all points with more detail, stories, and depth."
7. "Preaching Style" (Mic icon):
   - Instructs AI: "Please rewrite this in a more passionate and engaging preaching style."
8. "Telugu" (Globe icon):
   - Instructs AI: "Please translate the entire sermon to Telugu."

================================================================================
8. OUTPUT ACTIONS, EXPORTS & SOCIAL SHARING
================================================================================
8.1 Copy to Clipboard
---------------------
* Tapping "Copy":
  - Copies entire sermon text to mobile clipboard via `expo-clipboard`.
  - Displays an animated green checkmark notification: "Sermon copied to clipboard!".

8.2 WhatsApp Share Direct Integration
-------------------------------------
* Tapping "WhatsApp" (Green border with WhatsApp icon):
  - Automatically opens WhatsApp share intent with full sermon text pre-loaded in 
    the chat composer.
  - Allows instant sharing with associate pastors, translation teams, or worship leaders.

8.3 Plain Text Document Export & System Share
---------------------------------------------
* Saves sermon as a timestamped UTF-8 `.txt` file in device storage:
  `[Sermon_Title]_[Timestamp].txt`
* Automatically opens system sharing dialog allowing file export via Email, Google Drive, 
  Bluetooth, or AirDrop.

================================================================================
9. SERMON VAULT & HISTORY MANAGEMENT
================================================================================
Switching to the "Sermon History" tab opens the church's digital sermon archive:

9.1 Real-Time History Search Engine
-----------------------------------
* Search bar dynamically filters saved sermons by:
  - Title or Topic
  - Bible Reference (e.g., "John 3:16" or "యోహాను")
  - Sermon Body Text keywords

9.2 Full-Screen Sermon Reader Modal
-----------------------------------
* Tapping any historical sermon card opens the full-screen reader modal.
* Displays creation date, Scripture reference badge, and structured blocks.
* Bottom bar provides quick "Copy" and "Download" buttons.

9.3 Sermon Text Editor & Firestore Sync
---------------------------------------
* Tapping "Edit" opens a full-screen text editor modal.
* Preachers can edit the title, adjust points, add personal illustrations, and save.
* Automatically updates Firestore document and syncs across all devices.

9.4 Deletion with Destructive Confirmation
------------------------------------------
* Tapping the red Trash icon prompts a native alert:
  "Delete Sermon: Are you sure you want to delete '[Title]'? This action cannot be undone."
* Confirmed deletion purges the sermon from Firestore.

10. DATABASE SCHEMA & FIRESTORE INTEGRATION
================================================================================
Sermon records are stored in the Firestore collection `ai_sermons`:

Field                 | Type             | Description
----------------------+------------------+----------------------------------------------
id                    | string           | Unique document identifier
churchId              | string           | Associated church ID
title                 | string           | Sermon outline title
topic                 | string           | User-entered focus topic or selected situation
category              | string           | 'life' | 'topic'
language              | string           | 'Telugu' | 'English' | 'Hindi' | etc.
generatedSermonText   | string           | Complete markdown formatted sermon outline
bibleRef              | string           | Primary Scripture reference citation
status                | string           | 'generated' | 'edited'
createdAt             | timestamp        | Firestore server timestamp

================================================================================
11. BEST PRACTICES & PASTORAL GUIDELINES
================================================================================
1. The AI as an Assistant, Not a Preacher:
   - The AI Sermon Assistant is designed to break writer's block and organize thoughts. 
     Pastors should always bathe their preparation in personal prayer and study.
2. Scripture Verification:
   - Always verify Scripture passages with your physical Holy Bible or the app's 
     built-in Bible reader before preaching.
3. Language Switching:
   - If preaching a bilingual service, generate the outline in English first, review 
     the structure, and then tap the "Telugu" modifier chip to obtain the Telugu version.
