--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. In the Member App Home Tab, tap the "+ Enroll Child" button at the top right.
2. Form testing:

   f. Allergies: Tap allergen pills (e.g., "Dust / Asthma", "Peanuts").
   g. Authorized Pickup Guardians: Notice primary parent is pre-filled.
      Tap "+ Add Relative" to add Mother / Grandmother with phone number.

4. Expected Result: Alert appears: "Child Enrolled! 🎉 Ethan is registered for 
   Primary Stars! Sunday School custody pass is now active."
5. Tap OK. Ethan immediately appears in the family child switcher at the top.
