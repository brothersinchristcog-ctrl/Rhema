2. EXPENSE DASHBOARD & REAL-TIME FINANCIAL METRICS
================================================================================
When an admin opens the Expense tab, the Dashboard presents real-time numbers:

   • Sunday Service
   • Youth Meeting
   • Bible Study
   • Church Maintenance
   • Women's Prayer
   • Outreach / Gospel Missions

   Each card displays the count of expenses and the total amount in Rupees (₹).

3. RECORDING AN EXPENSE (PIN-TO-PIN STEP-BY-STEP)
================================================================================
Follow these exact steps to record any church expenditure:

STEP 1: INITIATE NEW EXPENSE
• Tap the floating "+ Add Expense" button or navigate to the "Editor" sub-tab.
• A modal or editor view opens with fresh blank fields.

STEP 2: SELECT MINISTRY CATEGORY
• Tap the Category Dropdown.
• Choose the relevant church department:
  - Sunday Service
  - Youth Meeting
  - Bible Study
  - Church Maintenance
  - Women's Prayer
  - Outreach
  - Or tap "+ Add Category" to create a new category (e.g., "VBS 2026").

STEP 3: SELECT LINE ITEMS
• Select pre-configured items from built-in groups (General, Vegetables, Groceries)
  or type a custom item name and tap "Add Item".
• Tapping an item highlights it with a blue border and adds it to the active
  line-item calculation table below.

STEP 4: ENTER QUANTITY & UNIT PRICE (PER ITEM)
• For each selected line item:
  - Quantity Field: e.g., 5 (kgs), 2 (units), 10 (packets), 1 (month)
  - Unit Price Field (₹): e.g., 50
• The system automatically multiplies: Subtotal = Quantity × Unit Price
• As you type, the Grand Total at the bottom updates in real-time.

STEP 5: ENTER VENDOR & PAYMENT DETAILS
• Vendor Name:
  - Enter shop, supplier, or contractor name (e.g. "Sri Rama Sound Systems",
    "Reliance Supermarket", "Brother David Travel Services").
• Expense Date:
  - Defaults to today's date. Tap the calendar icon to pick past service dates.
• Payment Method:
  - Choose one: Cash | UPI (GPay/PhonePe) | Cheque | Bank Transfer | Card
• Payment Status:
  - Choose "Paid" if already settled.
  - Choose "Pending" if the goods/services were taken on credit or bill is unpaid.
• Notes & Remarks:
  - Optional text: e.g., "Advance ₹1,000 paid; balance ₹2,000 due on Monday".

STEP 6: ATTACH PHYSICAL BILL / RECEIPT (OCR & ARCHIVING)
• Tap "Attach Bill / Document".
• Pick a photo of the paper receipt/vendor bill from camera or device storage.
• The document is linked to this expense record for audit verification.

STEP 7: SAVE EXPENSE
• Tap "Save Expense" (or "Update Expense" if editing).
• The record is written to Firestore: churches/{churchId}/expenses/{expenseId}
• The dashboard metrics, category totals, and expense lists refresh immediately.

5. DYNAMIC PRICE & QUANTITY CALCULATION ENGINE
================================================================================
• Formula per Item:
    Item Total (₹) = Quantity × Price Per Unit

• Grand Total Formula:
    Grand Total (₹) = Sum of all (Quantity × Price Per Unit) for all selected items

• Example:
    - Tea:        50 Cups  × ₹10  = ₹500
    - Biscuits:   10 Pkts  × ₹30  = ₹300
    - Water:       2 Cans  × ₹40  =  ₹80
    --------------------------------------
    GRAND TOTAL                   = ₹880

• Real-time responsiveness: Changing any number instantly recalculates the
  grand total with zero delay.

7. INVOICES & BILLING MANAGEMENT
================================================================================
Located under the "Invoices" sub-tab:

A. INVOICE GENERATION:
   1. Admin selects one or more recorded expenses from the list.
   2. Taps "Generate Invoice".
   3. The system assigns a unique formal Invoice Number:
      Format: INV-{ChurchCode}-{7-Digit-Sequential-Number}
      Example: INV-BIC-0000042
   4. Specifies:
      - Category (e.g. Sunday Service)
      - Invoice Date
      - Prepared By (e.g. Pastor / Treasurer Name)
      - Designated Approvers

B. HIGH-RESOLUTION BRANDED PDF EXPORT:
   The system generates an official A4-ready PDF containing:
   • Church Header: Legal Name, Logo, Registered Address, Phone Number
   • Invoice Meta: Invoice No, Invoice Date, Category, Prepared By, Reported By
   • Itemized Table: Item Name, Quantity, Unit Price (₹), Amount (₹)
   • Ministry Subtotals: Grouped by category
   • Grand Total Box in Indian Rupees (₹)
   • Official Footers: "Church Seal" box and "Authorized Signature" line
   • Wireless Printing: Send directly to any Wi-Fi printer via expo-print
   • Instant Sharing: Share directly via WhatsApp or Email via expo-sharing
   • Gallery Download: Save high-resolution PNG image directly to camera roll

8. THE MULTI-ADMIN INVOICE APPROVAL WORKFLOW
================================================================================
To ensure financial transparency and prevent unauthorized church spending:

STEP 1: SUBMITTING FOR APPROVAL
  • When generating an invoice, the submitter must select at least one
    Designated Approver from the registered Church Admins list.
  • Invoice status is set to "Pending Approval".

STEP 2: AUTOMATED NOTIFICATION TO APPROVERS
  • The system sends a high-priority push notification to the chosen Approvers:
    Title: "New Expense Invoice Approval"
    Content: "[User Name] submitted invoice (INV-BIC-0000042) for your approval."

STEP 3: REVIEW & DECISION BY APPROVER
  • The approver opens the notification or goes to Admin Finance -> Invoices.
  • The complete itemized invoice preview opens.
  • The approver has three options:
    [1] APPROVE:
        - Marks invoice status as "Approved".
        - Locks the invoice as an official approved church document.
    [2] REJECT:
        - Marks invoice status as "Rejected" with mandatory explanation comments.
    [3] REQUEST CHANGES:
        - Marks status as "Changes Requested" with specific feedback notes
          (e.g., "Please attach the transport petrol bill before approval").

STEP 4: NOTIFICATION BACK TO SUBMITTER
  • The submitter receives an automated push notification with the final decision:
    "Your expense invoice (INV-BIC-0000042) was [Approved/Rejected/Changes Requested]
     by [Approver Name]."

10. FIRESTORE DATABASE SCHEMA (TECHNICAL REFERENCE)
================================================================================

A. churches/{churchId}/expenses/{expenseId}
   {
     id: string,                 // Document ID
     category: string,           // 'Sunday Service' | 'Church Maintenance' | etc.
     date: string,               // 'YYYY-MM-DD'
     vendorName: string,         // 'City Supermarket'
     paymentMethod: string,      // 'Cash' | 'UPI' | 'Cheque' | 'Bank Transfer' | 'Card'
     status: 'Paid' | 'Pending', // Clearance status
     lineItems: [
       {
         type: string,           // 'Tea'
         quantity: number,       // 50
         pricePerUnit: number    // 10
       }
     ],
     amount: number,             // Total sum of all line items
     notes?: string,             // Remarks
     receiptUrl?: string,        // Storage URL of uploaded bill image
     churchId: string,
     createdAt: timestamp
   }

B. churches/{churchId}/invoices/{invoiceId}
   {
     id: string,                 // 'INV-BIC-0000042'
     category: string,
     date: string,
     amount: number,
     preparedBy: string,
     expenseIds: string[],       // Array of expense document IDs included
     status: 'Pending Approval' | 'Approved' | 'Rejected' | 'Changes Requested',
     reportedByUserIds: string[],// Selected approver admin IDs
     reportedByNames: string[],  // Selected approver admin names
     submitterPhone: string,
     approvalComments?: string,
     churchId: string,
     createdAt: timestamp
   }

C. churches/{churchId} (Root Document Fields for Finance)
   {
     customCategories: string[], // ['Christmas 2026', 'Building Expansion']
     customExpenseItems: {       // User-added items per group
       'General': ['Generator Rent', 'LED Screen'],
       'Vegetables': ['Capsicum', 'Mushroom'],
       'Groceries': ['Basmati Rice', 'Ghee']
     }
   }

11. SECURITY, ROLE PERMISSIONS & PUSH NOTIFICATIONS
================================================================================
• Role Security:
  - Only authenticated users with userType containing "ADMIN" or "SUPER"
    can access the Expense tab.
  - Regular members have zero visibility into church expenses or vendor bills.
• Offline / Online Sync:
  - Firestore caching enables offline draft entries; updates sync automatically
    when mobile data or Wi-Fi reconnects.
• Cross-Platform:
  - Works identically on both Android and iOS with native date pickers,
    sharing sheets, and printing bridges.
