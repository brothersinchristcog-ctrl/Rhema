1. MODULE OVERVIEW & VIRTUAL MINISTRY ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================
The Online Meetings module provides church leadership with a centralized broadcast 
and virtual classroom hub for conducting remote ministry beyond the physical church walls.

Common Use Cases:
* Mid-Week Online Bible Studies & Discipleship Classes
* Early Morning & Night Prayer Lines
* Youth Ministry Webinars & Q&A Panels
* Virtual Sunday School for Children
* Sunday Worship Webcasts for Sick or Traveling Congregation Members

Key Innovations:
* Native Google Meet Generator: Administrators can generate an official Google Meet 
  video space directly inside the app with a single tap through Google Sign-In OAuth, 
  without copying and pasting links.
* Universal Video Support: Full compatibility with Zoom, YouTube Live streams, 
  Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, or custom RTMP web links.
* Automated Church Broadcasts: Tapping "Start Class" dispatches an instant push notification 
  to every registered church member's smartphone: "🔴 Live Now: [Class Title] — Tap to join!".
* Real-Time Attendance Roster: Pastors can see exactly which church members are currently 
  present in the virtual classroom.

3. ONLINE MEETINGS DASHBOARD (AdminOnlineMeetings.tsx)
================================================================================
3.1 Header Navigation & Back Controls
-------------------------------------
* Deep royal navy header with white typography: "Online Meetings".
* Left chevron back button for seamless navigation.

3.2 Real-Time Stats Grid
------------------------
Four high-visibility metric cards calculating real-time status against the current time:
1. "UPCOMING": Total count of future scheduled meetings.
2. "LIVE NOW": Active meetings currently in session (`startTime <= now <= endTime`). 
   Renders in vibrant emerald green with an active pulse indicator.
3. "COMPLETED": Past meetings whose end time has elapsed.
4. "TOTAL CLASSES": Cumulative historical tally of all online sessions.

3.3 Status Filter Tabs
----------------------
A horizontal pill bar allowing quick filtering:
* "All": Shows all scheduled, active, and completed meetings.
* "Upcoming": Shows future sessions sorted chronologically.
* "Live": Isolates meetings currently broadcasting.
* "Completed": Displays past archives.

3.4 Meeting Feed & Card Anatomy
-------------------------------
Each meeting card in the list provides complete operational details:
* Live Status Pill: `🔴 LIVE NOW` (Emerald), `UPCOMING` (Blue), or `COMPLETED` (Slate).
* Title: e.g., "Wednesday Evening Discipleship Class".
* Description: Brief summary of the lesson or sermon outline.
* Date & Time Badge: e.g., "Wednesday, October 14 • 07:00 PM - 08:30 PM".
* Host Teacher: e.g., "Host: Rev. Dr. P. Johnson".
* Bible Focus: e.g., "Book of Romans — Chapter 8".
* Tapping any card opens its detailed pastoral management view.

4. CREATING & SCHEDULING A MEETING (AdminOnlineMeetingEditor.tsx)
================================================================================
Accessible via the "New Online Meeting" dashboard tile or tapping edit on an existing meeting.

4.1 Meeting Title & Description
-------------------------------
* Title Input (Required): e.g., "Youth Faith & Purpose Webinar".
* Description Input: Agenda, study questions, or preparation instructions for members.

4.2 Automated Google Meet Space Generator (OAuth Integration)
-------------------------------------------------------------
* "Generate Google Meet Link" Button with Google Meet icon:
  1. Initiates Google Sign-In OAuth flow (`@react-native-google-signin/google-signin`).
  2. Acquires authorized Google OAuth access token with scope:
     `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/meetings.space.created`
  3. Calls Google Meet REST API (`POST https://meet.googleapis.com/v2/spaces`) 
     with open access configuration.
  4. Receives unique `meetingUri` (e.g., `https://meet.google.com/abc-defg-hij`).
  5. Automatically populates the Meeting Link input and displays an animated toast: 
     "Link Generated! Google Meet link successfully created."

4.3 Manual Platform Links (Zoom, YouTube Live, MS Teams)
--------------------------------------------------------
* If the church hosts services on Zoom or YouTube:
  - Leaders can paste any valid web link directly into the "Meeting Link" text input:
    - Zoom: `https://us04web.zoom.us/j/1234567890?pwd=...`
    - YouTube Live: `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=...`
    - Microsoft Teams: `https://teams.microsoft.com/l/meetup-join/...`

4.4 Date & Time Pickers
-----------------------
* Meeting Date: Tap date box to open calendar modal (`DateTimePickerModal`).
* Start Time: Set start time (e.g., "07:00 PM").
* End Time: Set conclusion time (e.g., "08:30 PM").

4.5 Bible Book Focus & Curriculum Scripture
-------------------------------------------
* Text input to specify the Scripture focus (e.g., "Ephesians", "Gospel of John").
* Highlighted on member mobile devices so families can open their Bibles in advance.

4.6 Host / Teacher / Speaker Assignment
---------------------------------------
* Specify the minister or elder facilitating the meeting. Defaults to the logged-in pastor.

4.7 Save & Publish Action
-------------------------
* Tapping "Save Class / Meeting":
  - Commits the record to Firestore under the church's `online_meetings` collection.
  - Automatically notifies church members in their mobile app schedule.
  - Navigates back to the Online Meetings dashboard.

5. MEETING DETAIL VIEW & PASTORAL ACTION CONTROLS
================================================================================
When selecting any meeting from the dashboard, a comprehensive detail view opens:

5.1 Scheduled Meeting Detail Card
---------------------------------
* Dark navy card (`#22304F`) displaying:
  - Meeting Title & Description
  - Calendar Date (e.g., "Wednesday, October 14, 2026")
  - Time Window (e.g., "7:00 PM - 8:30 PM")
  - Scripture Reference (e.g., "Romans 8:1-17")
  - Host / Facilitator Name

5.2 "Start Class & Open Meet" Action
------------------------------------
* Large emerald green action button with Play icon: `Start Class & Open Meet`.
* Only active for upcoming or live meetings.
* Tapping this button triggers two simultaneous actions:
  1. Launches the meeting link directly in the device's Google Meet, Zoom, or YouTube app.
  2. Automated Push Broadcast (explained below).

5.3 Automated Church-Wide Live Broadcast Push Alert
---------------------------------------------------
Upon tapping "Start Class", the system writes a notification to the church's broadcast queue:
* Notification Title: `🔴 Live Now: [Meeting Title]`
* Notification Body: `The online meeting has started. Tap to join!`
* Payload: Attached direct video URL.
* All congregation members receive an audible chime on their smartphones; tapping the 
  alert immediately opens their video app and joins the meeting room!

5.4 Cancel Class Action
-----------------------
* Red outline button with X-Circle icon: `Cancel Class`.
* Prompts confirmation: "Are you sure you want to cancel this class?".
* Updates meeting status to `cancelled`, alerting members that the class will not take place.

5.5 Delete Class Record
-----------------------
* Ghost button with Trash icon: `Delete Class Record`.
* Permanently purges the class and all associated attendee logs from Firestore.

================================================================================
6. REAL-TIME LIVE ATTENDANCE TRACKER
================================================================================
A real-time pastoral attendance monitoring tool situated at the bottom of the detail view.

6.1 Live Attendance Counter
---------------------------
* Displays: `Live Attendance (X)` updating in real time as members join.

6.2 Real-Time Attendee Roster & Member Avatars
----------------------------------------------
* As congregation members tap "Join Meeting" in their mobile app:
  1. The app registers their attendance in the meeting's `attendees` subcollection.
  2. The admin dashboard receives an instant Firestore `onSnapshot` update.
  3. Displays a vertical list showing:
     - Member avatar circle with name initial (e.g., `[D]` for Daniel).
     - Full legal member name (e.g., "Bro. K. Daniel").
* Allows pastors to take roll call, acknowledge attendees by name, and follow up 
  with absent members.

7. DATABASE SCHEMA & FIRESTORE INTEGRATION
================================================================================
Online Meetings data is stored in the church's subcollections in Firestore:

1. Subcollection: `churches/{churchId}/online_meetings`
Field               | Type             | Description
--------------------+------------------+------------------------------------------------
id                  | string           | Unique meeting document auto-ID
title               | string           | Class or meeting title
description         | string           | Agenda, study notes, or context
meetingLink         | string           | Full Google Meet, Zoom, or YouTube URL
startTime           | timestamp        | Exact scheduled start timestamp
endTime             | timestamp        | Exact scheduled end timestamp
bibleBook           | string           | Scripture focus (e.g., 'Romans')
teacher             | string           | Host or teacher name
status              | string           | 'scheduled' | 'live' | 'completed' | 'cancelled'
createdAt           | timestamp        | Creation timestamp

2. Subcollection: `churches/{churchId}/online_meetings/{meetingId}/attendees`
Field               | Type             | Description
--------------------+------------------+------------------------------------------------
id                  | string           | Member document ID
name                | string           | Member full name
joinedAt            | timestamp        | Exact time member clicked join in mobile app

3. Collection: `churches/{churchId}/broadcasts`
Triggered automatically on "Start Class":
{
  title: "🔴 Live Now: Wednesday Bible Study",
  content: "The online meeting has started. Tap to join!",
  type: "online_meeting",
  targetChurchId: "church_123",
  meetingId: "meeting_456",
  url: "https://meet.google.com/abc-defg-hij",
  createdAt: serverTimestamp()
}

8. ONLINE MINISTRY BEST PRACTICES & STREAMING GUIDELINES
================================================================================
1. Test Links in Advance:
   - Always click the generated Google Meet or Zoom link 15 minutes before scheduled 
     start time to verify audio and video settings.
2. Leverage the Push Notification Broadcast:
   - Use the "Start Class & Open Meet" button inside the app rather than launching Meet 
     externally, as this automatically sends the instant reminder push notification to members.
3. Bible Focus Setting:
   - Always enter the Bible Book focus so congregation members can prepare their physical 
     scriptures or read the chapter before joining.
4. Attendance Follow-Up:
   - Review the Live Attendance roster after the meeting ends to identify regular attendees 
     and reach out to members who were unable to connect.
