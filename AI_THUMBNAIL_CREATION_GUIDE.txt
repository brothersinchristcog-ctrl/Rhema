1. MODULE OVERVIEW & DESIGN CAPABILITIES
================================================================================
The AI Thumbnail Creation Studio (AIContentCreator) is an enterprise-grade graphic 
design and content engine built specifically for churches, media teams, and pastors.

Instead of requiring complex third-party tools like Photoshop, Canva, or Illustrator, 
church leaders can create high-impact, professional 16:9 YouTube thumbnails, 
Facebook/Instagram banners, Sunday service posters, and convention flyers directly 
on their phone in less than 60 seconds.

Key Design Highlights:
* 16:9 Broadcast Format: Tailored for YouTube live streams, TV broadcasts, and social posts.
* Authentic Telugu Typography: Deep integration with Telugu Google Fonts ensuring vowels, 
  combining glyphs, and ligatures render flawlessly.
* Cultural Time Accuracy: First church app with automated Telugu period detection 
  (ఉదయం for morning, మధ్యాహ్నం for afternoon, సాయంత్రం for evening, and రాత్రి for night).
* Built-In Multilingual Bible: Direct access to 6 Bible translations (Telugu BSI/ERV and 
  English KJV/NIV/ESV/NKJV) with zero external searching.
* AI Generative Visuals: Integrates AI image models to produce reverent, sacred church 
  backgrounds without copyright concerns.

5. SERVICE SCHEDULE & SMART TELUGU TIME ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================
5.1 Start & End Date Pickers
----------------------------
* Tap date buttons to open native date pickers (`DateTimePickerModal`).
* Supports single-day services or multi-day conventions (e.g., 3-day Revival Meetings).

5.2 Duplicate-Free Date Range Formatter (formatDateRange)
---------------------------------------------------------
Intelligently avoids awkward date repetitions common in amateur posters:
* Single Day: "సెప్టెంబర్ 13, 2026" (Month name appears once).
* Same Month Multi-Day: "సెప్టెంబర్ 13 - 15, 2026" (Month name NEVER repeated twice).
* Cross-Month Multi-Day: "సెప్టెంబర్ 30 - అక్టోబర్ 2, 2026".

5.3 Start & End Time Pickers
----------------------------
* Time pickers with 12-hour AM/PM selection.

5.4 Accurate Telugu Time Periods (getTeluguTimePeriod)
------------------------------------------------------
Automatically detects and assigns the culturally correct Telugu period based on 
the 24-hour hour value:
* 04:00 - 11:59: "ఉదయం" (Morning)
* 12:00 - 15:59: "మధ్యాహ్నం" (Afternoon)
* 16:00 - 19:59: "సాయంత్రం" (Evening - e.g., 7:00 PM is సాయంత్రం)
* 20:00 - 03:59: "రాత్రి" (Night - e.g., 8:30 PM is night / రాత్రి!)

5.5 Multi-Period Time Range Formatter (formatTimeRange)
-------------------------------------------------------
Intelligently handles time intervals:
* Same Period: "సాయంత్రం 5:00 - 7:00" (Period appears once).
* Cross-Period: "సాయంత్రం 6:30 - రాత్రి 9:30" (Distinguishes evening from night).

5.6 Venue & GPS Pin Badge
-------------------------
* Venue Input: e.g., "Calvary Temple, Arundelpet, Guntur" or "Church Main Sanctuary".
* Rendered at the bottom of the poster canvas alongside a map pin icon.

6. MULTI-VERSION BIBLE SCRIPTURE ENGINE
================================================================================
Pastors can browse and insert exact Scripture verses directly into the poster.

6.1 6 Supported Bible Versions
------------------------------
1. Telugu (BSI) - బైబిల్ సొసైటీ ఆఫ్ ఇండియా (Standard classic translation)
2. Telugu (ERV) - సులభ వచన బైబిల్ (Easy-to-Read Version)
3. English (KJV) - King James Version (Reverent classic)
4. English (NIV) - New International Version (Modern clear)
5. English (ESV) - English Standard Version (Word-for-word)
6. English (NKJV) - New King James Version
* Plus integrated local Telugu Bible JSON for instant offline lookup.

6.2 3-Step Interactive Bible Navigator
--------------------------------------
* Step 1 (Book Modal): Lists all 66 books of the Old and New Testaments.
* Step 2 (Chapter Modal): Select chapter number.
* Step 3 (Verse Modal): Select starting verse number.
* The system instantly queries the Scripture engine and populates the verse text 
  and reference directly into the poster canvas!

6.3 66-Book Bilingual Search Engine
-----------------------------------
* The Book selection modal features a live search bar matching:
  - English names (e.g., "Genesis", "Romans", "Psalms")
  - Telugu names (e.g., "ఆదికాండము", "రోమీయులకు", "కీర్తనలు")

6.4 Direct Scripture Editing
----------------------------
* Both the Scripture verse text and reference citation fields are editable text areas, 
  allowing pastors to adjust phrasing, add emphasis, or combine multi-verse passages.

================================================================================
7.1 Authentic Telugu Google Fonts Library (loadTeluguFonts)

* Mandali: Smooth, flowing devotional typography.
* Gidugu: Stylized modern headline font.

* Noto Sans Telugu: Clean, legible sans-serif for body verses.
* Tenali Ramakrishna: Artistic, dramatic title font.

7.2 Font Size Controls & Stepper
--------------------------------
* Stepper buttons (`+` and `-`) and quick presets (Small, Medium, Large, Extra Large).
* Range: 8.0pt to 24.0pt with live canvas scaling.

7.3 Font Weight, Italic & Alignment
-----------------------------------
* Font Weight toggles: Regular, Medium, Semi-Bold, Bold (700, 800, 900).
* Italic toggle.
* Text Alignment: Left, Center, Right.

7.4 Vertical Text Positioning
-----------------------------
* Position switch: Top, Center, Bottom (allows moving title away from speaker photo).

7.5 Curated Text Color Palette & Luminance Contrast Engine
----------------------------------------------------------
* Palette includes: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`), Warm Gold (`#F3D98B`), Amber (`#F59E0B`), 
  Cyan (`#06B6D4`), Lime (`#84CC16`), and Custom HEX input.
* Automatic Luminance Contrast: Computes background luminance (`getLuminance`). If a dark 
  background is detected, ensures light text with drop shadows for 100% legibility.

13. BEST PRACTICES FOR HIGH-CLICKTHROUGH CHURCH POSTERS
================================================================================
1. Keep Headings Concise:
   - For YouTube thumbnails, 3-5 bold words in Telugu (e.g., "దేవుని అద్భుత కార్యం") 
     yield significantly higher click-through rates than long paragraphs.
2. High-Contrast Typography:
   - Use Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) or Warm Gold (`#F3D98B`) text over dark background 
     vignettes to ensure readability on small smartphone screens.
3. Accurate Time Periods:
   - Always verify that night meetings (8 PM onwards) reflect "రాత్రి" and evening 
     services (5 PM - 7 PM) reflect "సాయంత్రం" to prevent congregational confusion.
4. Speaker Framing:
   - Ensure the speaker's photo is a clean, front-facing portrait with good lighting.
