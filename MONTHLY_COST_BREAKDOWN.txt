2. DATABASE OPERATIONS (READS, WRITES, DELETES) & THEIR COSTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Google Firebase charges based on three simple operations:

A. READS (Viewing Data on Screen)
• What it is: Opening any screen that loads church data from the cloud.
• Examples:
  - Member opens app and reads 1 Daily Promise:  --> 1 Read
  - Member views 4 Church Events on the screen:  --> 4 Reads
  - Pastor views 25 members in Admin Dashboard:  --> 25 Reads
  - Member views 10 Prayer requests:             --> 10 Reads
• Google Free Allowance: 50,000 READS FREE EVERY DAY.
• Cost if exceeded: ~₹5.00 per 100,000 extra reads.

B. WRITES (Creating or Editing Data)
• What it is: Saving new data or editing existing data.
• Examples:
  - Pastor creates a new Promise, Sermon, or Event: --> 1 Write
  - A new member registers their profile:           --> 1 Write
  - A member updates their phone number or address: --> 1 Write
  - A member taps "Pray" on a prayer request:       --> 1 Write
  - A member records an online tithe/donation:      --> 1 Write
• Google Free Allowance: 20,000 WRITES FREE EVERY DAY.
• Cost if exceeded: ~₹15.00 per 100,000 extra writes.

C. DELETES (Removing Data)
• What it is: Permanently deleting an item.
• Examples:
  - Deleting an expired event or announcement:     --> 1 Delete
  - Removing an invalid member profile:            --> 1 Delete
• Google Free Allowance: 20,000 DELETES FREE EVERY DAY.
