HOW TO CREATE A PROMISE FROM THE ADMIN DASHBOARD
==================================================

1. Starting the Process
-----------------------
From the main Admin Dashboard, navigate to the "Promises" section and tap the "+ New" button in the top-right corner. This opens the Promise Editor with a fresh, blank form.


2. Schedule & Theme Section
---------------------------
- Promise Date (Required): Defaults to today's date. Tapping the date opens a calendar grid where you can pick any past date (for archive entry) or future date (for scheduled entry).
- Visual Theme & Background:
  * Curated Color & Gradients: Choose from 10 church-themed swatches (Deep Navy, Royal Purple, Forest Green, Burgundy, Amber Gold, Teal, Indigo, etc.) to set the background gradient for the promise card.
  * Live Preview Card: Displays exactly how the verse text and styling will appear on church members' phones in the 16:9 format.


3. English Promise
------------------
- Verse Reference (Required): Book, chapter, and verse (e.g., "John 3:16" or "Jeremiah 29:11").
- Verse Text (Required): Type or paste the English scripture text.
- Built-In Bible Picker: Tap "Select from Bible" to easily choose any book, chapter, and verse from the complete Old or New Testament without typing manually.
- Devotional Note (Optional): The Pastor's devotional thought, practical life application, or a short encouraging message for the day.


4. Telugu Promise
-----------------
- Verse Reference (Optional): Telugu reference (e.g., "యోహాను 3:16" or "యిర్మియా 29:11").
- Verse Text (Required): The full scripture text in Telugu.
- Built-In Telugu Bible Picker: Tap to select from all 66 books of the Telugu Holy Bible with authentic regional book names.
- Devotional Note (Optional): Pastor's commentary or prayer points written in Telugu.


5. Daily Promise Thumbnail
--------------------------
- Upload Custom Thumbnail: Tapping "Upload custom thumbnail" opens your device gallery. You can pick a custom 16:9 designed graphic.
- Clean Image Preservation: Uploaded custom photos are saved directly to Firebase Cloud Storage and displayed cleanly as-is without any unwanted overlays.


6. YouTube Link (Optional Video Devotion)
-----------------------------------------
- YouTube Video URL: Paste the full YouTube link (e.g., https://youtube.com/watch?v=... or https://youtu.be/...) OR the 11-character video ID.
- Video Title: The title of the devotion (e.g., "Morning Manna with Pastor David").
- Duration: Video length (e.g., "12 min").


7. Pastor & Publish Status
--------------------------
- Pastor Name: Credit the pastor or speaker giving the devotional (e.g., "Rev. Dr. P. Samuel").
- Publish Status (3 Interactive Cards):
  1. Publish Now (Green Card):
     - Becomes live on the Member App Home Screen hero carousel immediately.
  2. Scheduled (Amber Card):
     - Auto-publishes at 12:00 AM midnight on the selected "Promise Date".
  3. Save as Draft (Slate Card):
     - Saved privately in the database. Completely invisible to members until published.


8. Final Actions & What Happens Next
------------------------------------
A. Save as Draft:
- Tapping "Save as Draft" saves your work privately without notifying members.

B. Save & Publish:
- Tapping "Save & Publish Now" executes the live release.
- THE MAGIC STEP: An automated Push Notification broadcast is sent instantly to all church members' phones with the title:
  "📖 Daily Promise: [Reference]" and body: "[Verse Text] — [Reference]".
- Full-Screen Loading: A progress popup displays: "Saving & Publishing Daily Promise…".
- Celebratory Success Modal: Displays a glowing green badge, confirmation text, summary card, and a "View Promises List" button.
- REAL-TIME LIVE SYNC: The Member App Home Screen updates immediately with the new promise card in real-time.

HOW TO CREATE A SERMON FROM THE ADMIN DASHBOARD
===============================================

1. Starting the Process
-----------------------
From the main Admin Dashboard, navigate to the "Sermons" section and tap the "+ New" button in the top-right corner. This opens the Sermon Editor screen.


2. Sermon Info (Blue Card)
--------------------------
- Title — English (Required): The main display title (e.g., "Walking in Faith Through Trials").
- Title — Telugu (Optional): Telugu title for regional language congregation members (e.g., "విశ్వాసములో నడుచుకొనుట").
- Pastor / Speaker Name (Required): The name of the speaker (defaults to your church pastor, e.g., "Pastor Daniel Raju").
- Sermon Date: The date the message was preached or is scheduled for. Defaults to today; tap to modify via calendar.
- Duration: Length of the sermon (e.g., "45 min" or "1:15:00").
- Scripture Reference: The foundational scripture passage (e.g., "James 1:2-4").


3. Sermon Category & "+ New" Option (Blue Card)
-----------------------------------------------
- Categories List: Select all categories that apply to this message:
  * Sunday Service
  * Bible Study
  * Youth Meeting
  * Women's Fasting Prayer
  * Second Saturday Prayer
  * All-Night Prayer
  * Revival Meeting
  * Special Messages
  * Shorts / Testimonies
  Selected categories highlight in dark navy with a golden checkmark.

- THE "+ New" CATEGORY CREATOR:
  * Want to add a new category not in the list? (e.g., "Convention 2026", "Miracle Night", "VBS", "Cottage Prayer").
  * Tap the dashed [+ New] button.
  * A modal opens: enter the category name and tap "Create Category".
  * It instantly saves the category to the church database, auto-selects it for this sermon, makes it available for all future sermons, and shows it to Members in their category filter bar with an auto-assigned color pill.


4. Media & Details (Red Banner)
-------------------------------
- YouTube Video URL: Paste the full YouTube video link (e.g., https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=...), short link (https://youtu.be/...), or live stream ID.
- Custom Thumbnail (16:9 Landscape Banner):
  * Tap "Upload custom thumbnail" to pick a high-resolution JPG or PNG graphic from your device gallery.
  * It uploads securely to Firebase Cloud Storage under your church's folder.
  * Auto-Fallback: If no custom image is uploaded, the app automatically fetches the official YouTube high-quality thumbnail.
- Description: Multi-line field for an outline, summary notes, key points, or announcements related to the sermon.


5. Push Notification (Yellow Banner)
------------------------------------
- "Notify members when published" (Toggle Switch): Ensures members receive an alert when the sermon goes live.
- "Auto-send immediately on publish" (Toggle Switch): Dispatches notification immediately upon saving.
- Notification Preview: Displays a live lock-screen preview card showing how the notification will look on members' phones ("New Sermon 🎙️").


6. Publish Status (3 Interactive Cards)
---------------------------------------
Select one of the 3 status options:

1. Publish Now (Green Card `#10B981`):
   - Goes live immediately on the Member App Home Screen ("Latest Sermon") and Sermons Library.
   - Status badge shows a green radio checkmark.

2. Scheduled (Amber Card `#F59E0B`):
   - Queued to auto-publish on the specified Sermon Date.
   - Remains hidden from regular members until the scheduled date arrives.

3. Save as Draft (Slate Card `#64748B`):
   - Saved privately in the Admin panel.
   - Completely invisible to members. Perfect for drafting sermon notes or preparing upcoming series before publishing.


7. Final Actions & What Happens Next
------------------------------------
A. Save as Draft (White Button):
- Saves the sermon quietly with status set to "Draft".
- No push notifications are sent. Stored under the "Drafts" filter in Admin Sermon List.

B. Publish (Green / Navy Button):
- If "Publish Now" is selected:
  * The bottom button reads: [ Save & Publish Now ] in solid green.
  * THE MAGIC STEP: The app automatically sends a Push Notification broadcast to all church members:
    "🎧 New Sermon: [Title] — New sermon '[Title]' by [Pastor] is now available. Watch/listen now!"
- If "Scheduled" is selected:
  * The button reads: [ Save & Schedule ] in solid navy.
- Full-Screen Loading: A saving dialog appears ("Publishing Sermon… Syncing with Member View…").
- Celebratory Success Modal: Displays a triple-ring glowing badge, status pill, sermon details card (Title, Pastor, Scripture, Status), a primary "View Sermons List" button, and a "Keep Editing" option.
- REAL-TIME HOME SCREEN UPDATE: The Member App Home Screen "Latest Sermon" card updates immediately with the new sermon title, Telugu title, pastor name, formatted date, duration, and thumbnail artwork!
