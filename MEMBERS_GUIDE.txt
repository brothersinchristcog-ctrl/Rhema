HOW TO MANAGE, REGISTER & ORGANIZE CHURCH MEMBERS FROM THE ADMIN DASHBOARD
==========================================================================

1. Starting the Process
-----------------------
From the main Admin Dashboard, look under the "Community & Members" category:
- You will see the "Members" card featuring a full-width hero layout with a congregation users icon.
- Tapping "Members" opens the Church Members Directory & Pastoral Care Center (`AdminMembers.tsx`).
- Here pastors, church administrators, and elders oversee the church roll, track app usage, link household family trees, promote assistant leaders, and broadcast invitations.


=============================================================================================================================
2. MEMBERS DASHBOARD OVERVIEW & SCREEN LAYOUT (AdminMembers.tsx)
=============================================================================================================================

When you open the Members section, you are greeted with real-time membership intelligence:

A. Header Statistics Bar:
   - "Total Members": The total number of enrolled believers in the church directory.
   - "Active Members": Believers who regularly use the church app, attend services, and participate in church life.
   - "Inactive Members": Members who haven't opened the church app recently (highlights follow-up pastoral visits).
   - "App Installed Count": Tracks how many members have downloaded and logged into the mobile church app.

B. Top Header Options Menu (⋮ Icon at Top Right):
   - "Add Member": Opens the manual registration form to add an individual member with full profile details.
   - "Add From Contacts": Opens the Phonebook Bulk Importer to select and add multiple members straight from your device contacts.
   - "Export Members (CSV / Excel)": Downloads your entire church directory for offline backups and annual reporting.


=============================================================================================================================
3. SEARCH, FILTERS & VILLAGE / AREA GROUPING
=============================================================================================================================

Manage even large congregations of thousands of believers with high-speed filters:

A. Real-Time Search Bar:
   - Instant search as you type across:
     * Member Full Name
     * 10-Digit Mobile Phone Number
     * Residential Village / Town / Area
     * Email Address

B. Status Filter Pills:
   - `All`: Complete church directory in alphabetical order.
   - `Active`: Believers who actively use the church app.
   - `Inactive`: Believers who need a phone call or pastoral home visit.

C. Village / Area Dropdown Filter:
   - Automatically parses all unique villages and neighborhoods from member profiles (e.g., "Guntur", "Kakinada", "Old Town", "Sector 4").
   - Selecting a village filters the list to show only families residing in that area!
   - Ideal for organizing localized cottage prayer cells, fasting prayer homes, and area visitation routes.


=============================================================================================================================
4. ADDING A NEW MEMBER (+ Add Member Form) - COMPLETE FIELD-BY-FIELD GUIDE
=============================================================================================================================

When you tap "+ Add Member" or edit an existing believer, the Member Registration Form opens:

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Personal Information:
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- `Full Name` (Required):
  * The member's full legal or church baptismal name (e.g., "Daniel Raju", "Mary Margaret").
- `Mobile Phone Number` (Required):
  * 10-digit mobile number (e.g., "9876543210").
  * Serves as their primary login credential for the church mobile app.
  * Protected by automatic duplicate checking so no member is registered twice!
- `City / Village / Colony / Area` (Recommended):
  * The member's residential town, village, or neighborhood.
  * Powers the localized Village Filter across the church system.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
B. Administrative Role & Permissions:
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- `User Role / Access Level` (Dropdown):
  * "Member": Standard congregation believer with access to sermons, hymnal, daily promises, celebrations, and prayers.
  * "Admin": Co-pastor, church secretary, or media director with full access to the Admin Dashboard and all church management modules.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
C. Spiritual & Family Milestones (Automated Celebrations Feeder):
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
These dates feed directly into the WeChristian automated Church Celebrations module:
- `Date of Birth (DOB)` (Date Picker):
  * Tap the calendar icon to select their birthday (`DateTimePickerModal`).
  * On their birthday, the app automatically greets them, alerts the pastor to pray, and enables church members to bless them!
- `Water Baptism Date` (Date Picker):
  * Records the exact spiritual milestone when the believer was baptized in water.
- `Wedding Anniversary Date` (Date Picker):
  * For married believers, record their marriage anniversary to celebrate each year with pastoral blessings.

Action Buttons:
- "Save Member" (Green Button): Validates phone number and name, saves to Firestore, and updates live church counts.
- "Cancel" (X Icon): Closes the form.


=============================================================================================================================
5. MEMBER CARD ANATOMY & ONE-TAP PASTORAL TOOLS
=============================================================================================================================

Every member card in the church directory provides rich, one-tap pastoral oversight:

A. Visual Profile Header:
   - Member Profile Photo: Displays uploaded photo or clean fallback initials avatar with church theme colors.
   - Full Name in bold typography.
   - Phone Number and Residential Village.
   - Status Badge: Green ("Active"), Gray ("Inactive"), or Gold ("Admin").

B. One-Tap Pastoral Contact Buttons:
   - Call Icon (Green Button): Initiates an immediate phone call (`tel:...`) directly from the dashboard.
   - WhatsApp Icon (Green WhatsApp Button): Opens WhatsApp with an instant chat window to send encouraging scriptures or pastoral check-ins.

C. Expandable Profile Details (Tap to Expand):
   - Tapping any member card reveals their complete spiritual profile:
     * Date of Birth (DOB) and age calculation.
     * Wedding Anniversary Date.
     * Water Baptism Date.
     * App Status: "App Installed" (with last active timestamp) vs "Not Yet Installed".

D. Household Family Tree & Linked Relatives:
   - WeChristian automatically links believers belonging to the same family or household!
   - Tapping "View Family" expands the household list showing spouse, children, and parents linked under that family ID.

E. Role Management (Promoting & Revoking Admins):
   - Tap the 3 dots (⋮) on any member card:
     * "Promote to Admin": Promotes a trusted elder, youth leader, or associate pastor to Admin status. They immediately receive Admin Dashboard privileges!
     * "Remove Admin Role": Safely reverts permissions back to standard member status at any time.

F. Delete Member (Trash Icon):
   - Opens a custom confirmation dialog: *"Are you sure you want to delete [Member Name] from the church roll?"*.
   - Safely removes the member record and updates congregation statistics.


=============================================================================================================================
6. ADDING MEMBERS VIA "ADD FROM CONTACTS" (InviteMembersModal.tsx)
=============================================================================================================================

Tapping "Add From Contacts" from the top menu allows pastors to bulk-import church members straight from their smartphone address book without manual typing:

A. Phonebook Permission & High-Speed Loading:
   - Asks for device contact permission once, then instantly loads all contacts from your phone with valid mobile numbers.

B. Search & Multi-Selection:
   - Live Search: Type any name or phone number to quickly locate church believers in your contacts.
   - "Select All" / "Deselect All": One-tap buttons to select hundreds of contacts at once.
   - Selection Counter: Shows live count (e.g., "45 Contacts Selected").

C. Automatic Import & Duplicate Protection:
   - When you proceed, WeChristian automatically adds every selected contact into your church's database in Firestore.
   - Duplicate Guard: Compares phone numbers against existing members so existing members are NEVER duplicated!
   - Real-Time Sync: The dashboard's Total Members count updates immediately.

D. Sending Welcome Message (SMS / WhatsApp):
   - "Send SMS": Dispatches a personalized SMS message with your church app link and unique church code.
   - "WhatsApp": Opens WhatsApp with a pre-formatted invitation message welcoming them to the church app with login instructions!


=============================================================================================================================
7. HOW CONGREGATION MEMBERS USE THE DIRECTORY IN THE APP (MembersScreen.tsx)
=============================================================================================================================

When church believers open the "Members" or "Family" section on their phone:
1. Family Profile & Household Tree:
   - Members view their personal profile and manage their immediate household.
   - Tap "+ Add Family Member" to register their spouse, children, or elderly parents.
2. Relationship Tagging:
   - Members can tag family roles: Husband, Wife, Father, Mother, Son, Daughter, Brother, Sister, Grandfather, Grandmother.
3. Pastoral Care Connection:
   - Believers can view church pastoral staff, elders, and emergency prayer team contacts to request prayer or pastoral visitation when needed.
