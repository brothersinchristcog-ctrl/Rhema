1. OVERVIEW & ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================
The Daily Promise system allows church administrators to publish daily
scripture verses, devotional commentary, customized 16:9 branded graphics,
and YouTube devotional videos.

When a Daily Promise is published:
- It appears immediately on the Member App Home Screen hero card.
- It appears in the Daily Video / Devotional Player screen.
- It is permanently recorded in the Church Promise Archive.
- An automated push notification broadcast is sent to all church members.

2. PART 1: THE "PROMISES" TAB (DASHBOARD & LIST VIEW)
================================================================================

[ TOP HEADER BAR ]
* "< Back" Button:
  - Returns to the main Admin Dashboard.
* "Daily Promises" Title:
  - Header of the screen.
* "+ New" Button:
  - Opens the New Promise Editor with a fresh blank form.

[ THREE INTERACTIVE CLICKABLE STAT CARDS ]
All three summary stat cards at the top are interactive filters:

1. "Published" Card (Moss Green):
   - Shows total count of promises live with status 'Published'.
   - CLICK ACTION: Filters the list to display all Published promises in
     reverse-chronological order.
   - Tapping it again resets the filter to show all.

2. "Drafts" Card (Gold):
   - Shows total count of promises saved as private 'Draft'.
   - CLICK ACTION: Instantly filters the list to show all unpublished drafts,
     allowing you to quickly locate, edit, and publish draft work.
   - Tapping it again resets the filter to show all.

3. "Missing" Card (Terracotta Clay):
   - Shows count of empty days in the current month without a promise.
   - CLICK ACTION: Displays the full calendar grid of all missing dates with
     direct "+ Add" buttons for each missing day.
   - Tapping it again resets the filter to show all.

[ ACTIVE FILTER BANNER ]
* When any stat card is active, a filter banner appears:
  - Displays: "Showing: Published Promises (X)" or "Draft Promises (X)".
  - "Show All" Button: One-tap button to clear the filter and restore the full
    overview.

[ DEFAULT SECTIONS (WHEN NO FILTER IS ACTIVE) ]
* Alert Banner:
  - Displays when days in the current month are still unassigned.
* "Today" Section:
  - Shows today's active promise. If empty, displays a "+ Schedule today's
    promise" button.
* "Upcoming" Section:
  - Lists future promises in chronological order.
* "Missing Dates" Preview:
  - Displays cards for empty calendar days with month, day, and "+ Add" button.
  - Includes "View all (X) ->" button.
* "Past Promises" Section:
  - Shows previously published promises for historical reference.

[ INDIVIDUAL PROMISE CARDS IN LIST ]
Each card displayed contains:
* Header Band:
  - Date (e.g., 14 September 2026).
  - Status Badge Pill: Green "Published", Blue "Scheduled", or Amber "Draft".
  - Seal Badge: Checkmark (past/today) or Plus icon (upcoming).
* Body:
  - Scripture Reference: English and Telugu citations (e.g. John 3:16 | యోహాను 3:16).
  - Pastor Name: Displayed with a bullet.
  - English Verse: Editorial style with drop-cap.
  - Telugu Verse: Native Telugu scripture text.
* Bottom Toolbar:
  - Flags: English, Telugu, and YouTube indicators.
  - "Edit ->" Button: Opens the promise in the editor with all data loaded.

[ FLOATING ACTION BUTTON (FAB +) ]
* Located in the bottom-right corner. Tapping it opens the New Promise editor.

3. PART 2: THE "NEW PROMISE / EDIT PROMISE" TAB (EDITOR)
================================================================================

[ SECTION 1: SCHEDULE ]
* "Promise date *" Field:
  - Displays currently selected date.
  - Tapping opens a calendar date picker to pick any day of the month.

[ SECTION 2: ENGLISH PROMISE & AUTO-POPULATE BIBLE ENGINE ]
* Auto-Populate Bible Verse Box:
  - "Select Book": Picker with all 66 books of the Bible in English & Telugu.
  - "Select Chapter": Dynamically loads chapters for the chosen book.
  - "Select Verse": Dynamically loads verse numbers for the chosen chapter.
  - Auto-Fetch Engine: Automatically queries Bolls KJV Bible API and local
    Telugu Bible simultaneously, populating:
      1. English reference (e.g., John 3:16)
      2. English verse text
      3. Telugu reference (e.g., యోహాను 3:16)
      4. Telugu verse text
* "Scripture reference *" Input:
  - Manual edit or auto-filled citation.
* "Verse text (English) *" Textarea:
  - Full English scripture text.
* "Devotional Note (English)" Textarea:
  - Optional commentary or prayer points displayed to members.

[ SECTION 3: TELUGU PROMISE (తెలుగు వాగ్దానము) ]
* "Scripture reference (Telugu)" Input:
  - Telugu citation (e.g., యోహాను 3:16).
* "Verse text (Telugu)" Textarea:
  - Native Telugu scripture text with custom serif typography.

[ SECTION 4: DEVOTIONAL THUMBNAIL (16:9 LANDSCAPE CARD) ]
Every promise generates a branded 16:9 graphic displayed on Member Home View:

1. Live 16:9 Graphic Preview:
   - Header Bar: Church Logo or Cross, Church Name, and Date Pill.
   - Center Card: Scripture text in quotation marks with golden reference pill.
   - Footer Bar: YouTube video badge, Pastor name, and Church branding.

2. Media Control Buttons:
   - "Regenerate" Button: Cycles through high-resolution Christian landscape
     backgrounds.
   - "Change Background" Button: Opens device gallery to upload custom church
     photos.
   - "AI Redesign" Button: AI-enhances the visual composite.

3. Thumbnail Colors & Custom Palette (Collapsible via Chevron):
   - Color Family Tabs: Filter swatches by Blue, Navy, Purple, Violet, Pink,
     Red, Orange, Gold, Green, Teal, Slate, etc.
   - Swatches: Over 50 curated tones and gradients.
   - Custom Color Mode:
     - Solid: Enter any Hex code or tap a spectrum dot.
     - Gradient: Enter custom Start & End Hex colors with live preview bar.

[ SECTION 5: VIDEO DEVOTIONAL (OPTIONAL) ]
* "Devotional Video Title" Input: Title for the devotional video.
* "Duration" Input: Length hint (e.g., 1:20).
* "YouTube video URL" Input: Full YouTube link or video ID. When provided,
  a video player card appears for members.

[ SECTION 6: PASTOR & PUBLISH STATUS ]
* "Pastor Name" Input: Name of the minister sharing the promise.
* Publish Status (Dedicated 3-Card Selector):
  1. "Publish Now" Card (Emerald Green):
     - Icon: Send.
     - Subtext: "Live immediately on Member Home View".
     - Action: Publishes immediately and broadcasts push notification.
  2. "Scheduled" Card (Amber / Navy):
     - Icon: Clock.
     - Subtext: "Auto-publishes on [Selected Date]".
     - Action: Queued to automatically show when that calendar date arrives.
  3. "Save as Draft" Card (Slate):
     - Icon: Document.
     - Subtext: "Saved privately, hidden from members".
     - Action: Saved to database archive without being visible to members.

[ SECTION 7: ACTION BUTTONS & FAB ]
* Bottom Action Buttons (Dynamically adapts to selected status):
  - When status is "Publish Now":
    * Left: "Save as Draft" (saves draft).
    * Right (Primary): "Save & Publish Now" (Green #15803D).
  - When status is "Scheduled":
    * Left: "Save as Draft".
    * Right (Primary): "Save & Schedule" (Navy #1a2d5a).
  - When status is "Draft":
    * Left: "Publish Now" (Green #15803D, quick publish).
    * Right (Primary): "Save as Draft" (Slate #475569).
* "<- Back to list" Button:
  - Cancels and returns to Promises list.
* Floating Action Button (FAB):
  - Bottom-right corner circular quick-save button. Icon and background color
    automatically adapt to status (Send for Published, Clock for Scheduled,
    Save for Draft).

================================================================================
4. PART 3: SAVING, SUCCESS FEEDBACK & LIVE MEMBER APP SYNC
================================================================================

[ SAVING PROCESS ]
1. Captures the high-resolution 16:9 composite graphic via ViewShot.
2. Uploads the final graphic to cloud storage.
3. Saves the complete promise details to Firestore.
4. Clears local device cache (@cached_daily_promise) so the Member Home View
   immediately reflects the newest update.
5. If status is "Published", broadcasts a push notification to all members.

[ CELEBRATORY SUCCESS MODAL ]
* Decorative Top Gradient Accent Bar:
  - Emerald for Published, Amber/Navy for Scheduled, Slate for Draft.
* Layered Multi-Ring Glowing Icon Badge:
  - Animated multi-ring glow with central icon (CheckCircle2 / Clock / FileText).
* Status Pill Badge:
  - "LIVE ON MEMBER APP" / "SCHEDULED * [Date]" / "SAVED AS PRIVATE DRAFT".
* Headline & Explanatory Text:
  - "Promise Published! 🎉" / "Promise Scheduled! ⏰" / "Draft Saved! 💾".
* Summary Details Card:
  - Verse citation, Date, and Pastor Name.
* Dual Action Buttons:
  - Primary: "View Promises List ->" with LinearGradient to navigate to list.
  - Secondary: "Stay & Keep Editing" link to dismiss and continue editing.
