HOW TO MANAGE, PUBLISH & ORGANIZE WORSHIP SONGS FROM THE ADMIN DASHBOARD
========================================================================

1. Starting the Process
-----------------------
From the main Admin Dashboard, look under the "Content Management" category:
- You will see the "Songs" card featuring a full-width hero layout with a music note icon.
- Tapping "Songs" opens the Church Worship Hymnal Studio (`AdminSongEditor.tsx`).
- Here pastors, worship leaders, and choir directors can manage church songs, chord keys, lyrics in Telugu and English, and worship setlists.


=============================================================================================================================
2. SONG DIRECTORY TABS & SCREEN LAYOUT (AdminSongEditor.tsx)
=============================================================================================================================

When you open the Songs section, you have 3 distinct library views at the top:

A. "All Songs" (`list` Tab):
   - Comprehensive hymnal combining both the Global Master Song Library and your church's local songs.
   - Alphabetical A-Z sorting for rapid indexing.

B. "Theme Songs" (`theme` Tab):
   - Curated songs for special church themes (e.g., Annual Conventions, Youth Fests, Revival Weeks, Church Anniversary).

C. "Our Church Songs" (`churchOwn` Tab):
   - Filters exclusively the original songs and localized hymns added by your specific church.


=============================================================================================================================
3. CATEGORIES & MUSIC FILTERS
=============================================================================================================================

A. 12 Core Worship Categories:
   - "Stuthi Songs" (Praise / Thanksgiving hymns)
   - "Aradhana Songs" (Deep Adoration & Worship hymns)
   - "Offering Songs" (Tithes & Offertory collection hymns)
   - "Christmas Songs" (Advent & Nativity carols)
   - "Easter Songs" (Resurrection & Good Friday hymns)
   - "Youth Songs" (Contemporary, upbeat praise songs)
   - "Gospel Songs" (Evangelistic & Outreach songs)
   - "Marriage Songs" (Wedding celebration hymns)
   - "Thanksgiving Songs" (Testimonies & Harvest festival)
   - "Special Songs" (Solo / Choir specials)
   - "Theme Songs" (Annual ministry mottos)
   - "Other" (General devotional songs)

B. Alphabet Ribbon (A-Z + All):
   - Tap any letter from 'A' through 'Z' to instantly jump to songs starting with that letter.

C. Live Search Bar:
   - Search by English Song Title, Telugu Song Title, Artist / Lyricist name, or any lyric phrase!


=============================================================================================================================
4. POSTING A NEW SONG (+ Add Song Form) - COMPLETE FIELD-BY-FIELD GUIDE
=============================================================================================================================

When you tap "+ Add Song", the Song Creation Modal opens with the following fields:

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Song Titles (Bilingual Support):
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- `Song Title (English)` (Required):
  * Romanized title commonly searched by youth and musicians (e.g., "Hosanna Hosanna Sarvonnathuda", "Yehova Naa Moranu").
- `Song Title (Telugu)` (Recommended):
  * Authentic Telugu script title (e.g., "హోసన్నా హోసన్నా సర్వోన్నతుడా").
  * Displayed in bold for congregation members who read Telugu hymnals.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
B. Artist, Author & Composer:
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- `Artist / Songwriter / Composer` (Optional):
  * Credits the author or musician (e.g., "Bro. Yesanna", "P.D. Sundara Rao", "Pastor A.R. Stevenson", "Traditional").

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
C. Musical Scale & Chord Key:
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- `Musical Key / Scale` (Dropdown):
  * Select the primary singing key to guide the choir and worship keyboardists/guitarists:
    - Major Keys: C, C#, D, Eb, E, F, F#, G, Ab, A, Bb, B
    - Minor Keys: Cm, C#m, Dm, Ebm, Em, Fm, F#m, Gm, Abm, Am, Bbm, Bm
  * Displays a prominent Key Badge on the song card for easy reference on stage!

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D. Worship Categories Selection:
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- `Category Checklist` (Multi-select):
  * Tag the song under one or more categories (e.g., tag a hymn as both "Stuthi Songs" and "Youth Songs").
  * Songs appear automatically under each tagged category filter.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E. Reference Video & Demo Link:
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- `YouTube Reference URL / Video ID` (Optional):
  * Paste a YouTube link of the reference worship song.
  * Allows church choir members to listen to the tune and learn harmony parts directly in the app!

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F. Full Song Lyrics (Stanzas & Chorus):
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- `Song Lyrics` (Required - Multi-line Rich Text Field):
  * Enter the complete lyrics formatted with clear stanzas:
    - Pallavi / Chorus
    - Anupallavi
    - Charanam 1 (Stanza 1)
    - Charanam 2 (Stanza 2)
  * Supports Telugu script, English transliteration, or bilingual split view.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
G. Publication Status:
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- `Status`:
  * "Published": Instantly visible to the entire congregation in their app songbook.
  * "Draft": Kept in private admin drafts for review before releasing to the church.

Action Buttons:
- "Publish Song" (Green Button): Validates entries, syncs to Firestore database, and clears the form.
- "Cancel / Discard" (X Icon): Closes modal without saving.


=============================================================================================================================
5. SONG CARD ACTIONS & SONG DETAIL MODAL (SongDetailModal.tsx)
=============================================================================================================================

In the song list, each song card displays the Title (English & Telugu), Category badge, Key scale, and Action buttons:

A. Tap Song Card (View Lyrics):
   - Opens the full-screen Lyric Viewer (`SongDetailModal.tsx`):
     * Distraction-free clean reading view with large, clear font.
     * Musical Key badge at the top (e.g., "Key: D Major").
     * Clean line spacing between Pallavi and Charanams.
     * Quick-share button to send lyrics directly to WhatsApp choir groups!

B. Edit Song (Pencil Icon):
   - Opens the Edit Modal pre-populated with existing lyrics, scale, and categories.
   - Allows instant correction of spelling or adding additional stanzas.

C. Delete Song (Trash Icon):
   - Prompts a confirmation dialog: "Are you sure you want to delete this song?".
   - Upon confirmation, safely deletes the song and displays a green success confirmation toast.

D. Star / Favorite ("My Songbook"):
   - Members and choir leaders can tap the Star icon to bookmark songs into their personal "My Songbook" setlist for Sunday services.


=============================================================================================================================
6. GLOBAL MASTER HYMNAL SYNC (SUPER ADMIN INTEGRATION)
=============================================================================================================================

WeChristian provides a dual-tier hymnal structure:
1. Global Master Songs:
   - Curated by Platform Super Admins with hundreds of standardized Telugu Christian hymns and worship classics.
   - Automatically available to every church organization out-of-the-box.
2. Local Church Custom Songs:
   - Original worship compositions, regional folk lyrics, and youth choruses added directly by your local church pastoral team.
   - Automatically distinguished with an "Our Church" badge.


=============================================================================================================================
7. HOW CONGREGATION MEMBERS & CHOIR USE SONGS IN THE APP
=============================================================================================================================

When church believers and musicians open the "Songs" tab on their phone:
1. Digital Hymnal in Church:
   - Believers follow along during Sunday worship services without needing physical paper songbooks.
2. Fast Number & Alphabet Lookup:
   - Type song number or first Telugu word to open lyrics in 1 second.
3. Worship Key Display for Musicians:
   - Keyboardists and guitarists instantly see the original singing scale (e.g., "Key: E").
4. "My Songbook" Personal Setlists:
   - Choir teams bookmark the 4-5 songs selected for Sunday morning into their personal favorites tab for instant access on stage.
5. WhatsApp Sharing:
   - Members can share full song lyrics with church prayer groups or family members with one tap.
