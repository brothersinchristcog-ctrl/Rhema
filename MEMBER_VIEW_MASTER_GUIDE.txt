1. ARCHITECTURAL PHILOSOPHY & THE MEMBER EXPERIENCE
================================================================================
While the WeChristian Admin Console is focused on governance, database entries,
financial audits, and moderation, the Member View is designed as an intimate,
spiritually inspiring digital sanctuary.

Every screen in the Member View is crafted to eliminate friction between a
believer and their local church:
  - Zero Clutter: Clean, distraction-free typography utilizing Georgia/Serif
    for scriptures and crisp Sans-Serif for functional controls.
  - Native Bilingual Design: Complete first-class support for Telugu and English
    across verses, hymnals, sermon titles, and navigation labels.
  - Real-Time Pastoral Connection: Direct bridges for prayer, WhatsApp
    outreach, online worship services, and attendance check-ins.
  - Offline Resilience: Built-in local caching ensuring the Bible, daily
    promises, and saved notes remain fully accessible even without internet.

2. ONBOARDING, CHURCH JOINING & BIOMETRIC SECURITY
================================================================================

2.1 APP LAUNCH & SPLASH SCREEN ANIMATION
  - Component: `SplashScreen.tsx`
  - When the app is launched, a 2.5-second brand splash sequence activates.
  - Reads stored credentials from `@react-native-async-storage/async-storage`
    and Firebase Auth state.
  - Proactive Self-Healing: If a member signs in with a phone number that exists
    in the church's master member roster but lacks a local profile document,
    `RootNavigator` queries `FirestoreService.checkContactExists` and
    automatically repairs the profile document on the fly without user disruption.

2.2 CHURCH CODE DISCOVERY & MULTI-CAMPUS JOINING
  - Component: `OnboardingScreen.tsx`
  - First-time visitors and new believers are prompted to enter their church's
    unique alphanumeric Join Code (e.g. `COGH001`, `BICGUNTUR`).
  - Validation: Queries Firestore collection `churches` where
    `churchCode == enteredCode.toUpperCase()`.
  - Once joined:
    * The member is instantly bound to that specific local congregation.
    * The app downloads the church's custom brand colors, logo, and active features.
    * In multi-campus setups, the member is seamlessly routed to their regional
      branch.

2.3 BIOMETRIC APP LOCK (FINGERPRINT / FACE ID)
  - Service: `SecurityService.ts`
  - Members can activate Biometric Security inside Profile Settings.
  - AppState Awareness:
    * When the WeChristian app is minimized or backgrounded and returned to the
      foreground, the app instantly engages the lock screen (`isLocked: true`).
    * Displays the gold shield lock prompt with church name branding.
    * Invokes `expo-local-authentication` (TouchID, FaceID, or Android BiometricPrompt).
    * Upon successful verification, the lock state unlocks smoothly.

2.4 GUEST MODE VS. AUTHENTICATED COVENANT MEMBER
  - The app permits seekers and visitors to explore in "Guest Mode" without
    entering a phone number.
  - Guest Permissions:
    * Can view Home, Daily Verses, Church Bio, Contact Us, and public Sermons.
  - Covenant Guard (`handleGuestProtectedNavigation`):
    * Tapping interactive community modules (Giving, Prayer Wall, Member Directory,
      Attendance, Notes, Songs) triggers an elegant prompt:
      "Sign In Required: Please sign in with your phone number to access community features."

3. DYNAMIC FLOATING PILL BOTTOM TAB BAR (5 CORE HUBS)
================================================================================

3.1 FLOATING PILL DESIGN & DYNAMIC COLOR MORPHING
  - Component: `CustomTabBar` in `RootNavigator.tsx`
  - Position: Elevated floating bar positioned 50px (Android) / 65px (iOS) above
    the bottom edge, with 40px border radius, high-elevation drop shadow, and
    horizontal padding.
  - Dynamic Color Morphing:
    The background color of the pill bar dynamically morphs based on which tab
    is currently active to reflect the mood of that ministry hub:
    * Tab 1: Home     -> Deep Navy (`#1a2d5a`)
    * Tab 2: Promise  -> Royal Teal (`#0F766E`)
    * Tab 3: Sermons  -> Sunset Orange (`#D8632E`)
    * Tab 4: Prayer   -> Sky Blue (`#0284C7`)
    * Tab 5: Profile  -> Slate Charcoal (`#27272A`)
  - Active Tab Animation:
    The focused tab transforms into a crisp white circular badge (`activeCircle`),
    popping the icon forward with high-contrast colored iconography and bold label.

3.2 DYNAMIC NOTIFICATION BADGES & PULSING INTERCESSION RING
  - Monitored Collection: `churches/{churchId}/prayerRequests`
    (where `isPublic: true` and `isAnswered: true`).
  - When new answered prayers or community requests arrive that the member has
    not yet seen:
    * An animated pulse ring (`styles.pulseRing`) pulses behind the Heart icon.
    * A vibrant red count pill displays the number of unseen prayer requests.
    * Tapping the Prayer tab marks prayers as read in `@LastSeenPrayerCount` and
      navigates directly to the public request stream.

11. MEMBER TROUBLESHOOTING & FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
================================================================================
Q1: How do I change the language from English to Telugu?
A: Tap the "EN / తె" button in the top right corner of the Home screen, or open
   the Profile tab and select "Language".

Q2: Can I read the Bible without an internet connection?
A: Yes! The entire Holy Bible (both Old and New Testaments in Telugu and English)
   is stored locally on your device for complete offline reading.

Q3: Is my financial donation secure?
A: Yes. All online donations are processed directly through RBI-compliant,
   bank-grade 256-bit encrypted Razorpay payment gateways and official NPCI
   UPI protocols. WeChristian never stores credit card or debit card numbers.

Q4: How do I share today's promise on WhatsApp?
A: Open the Home screen or Promise tab, and tap the `[Save Card]` or `[Share]`
   button. The app will generate a custom image card and open WhatsApp
   automatically.
