## 1. Onboarding & Profile

### Registration & Login
When a member opens the WeChristian app for the first time:
- **Join Code:** Members enter your church's unique Join Code (e.g., `MAIN001`). This links their app directly to your church's database and loads your custom branding (colors, logo, banner).
- **Phone Login:** Members log in securely via OTP to their registered phone number.
- **Account Creation:** If they are a new user, they will be prompted to enter their Name, DOB, and City/Village.

### Profile Screen
Accessed via the Avatar icon in the top corner.
- **Digital ID:** Displays a digital church membership card.
- **Edit Details:** Members can update their profile picture, email address, physical address, and dates (Birthday, Baptism, Anniversary).
- **Household Management:** Members can add spouses and children to their household to group families together in the Admin directory.
- **Theme Preferences:** Members can toggle Dark Mode or Light Mode for their app.

## 2. Home Dashboard
The Home Screen is the central hub of the app, dynamically loading content based on the day.

### The Daily Promise
- Front and center is the **Daily Promise**. This displays the scripture and devotional assigned by the Pastor for today.
- **Action:** Tap the Promise card to read the full Telugu/English text, or tap the video thumbnail (if attached) to watch the daily devotional video on YouTube.
- **Archive:** Members can swipe or tap "View Past Promises" to see an archive of previous daily scriptures.

### Quick Actions Grid
A set of shortcut buttons allowing one-tap access to frequently used features:
- **Bible**, **Sermons**, **Songs**, **Give**, **Prayer**, and **Events**.

### Feed & Highlights
- **Active Attendance:** If an Admin sends an active attendance request, a large "Roll Call" banner appears at the top. Members tap **Yes** or **No** (with an optional reason).
- **Recent Sermons:** A horizontal scrolling list of the latest audio/video sermons.
- **Upcoming Events:** Cards showing the next 3 events, along with a countdown timer.
- **Live Now:** If an Online Meeting is currently active, a red "🔴 Live" card appears, allowing members to join instantly.

## 3. Spiritual Growth

### 📖 The Bible Module
A fully functional, offline-capable digital Bible built right into the app.
- **Translations:** Switch between English and Telugu (or side-by-side).
- **Navigation:** Browse by Old/New Testament, Book, and Chapter.
- **Reader Mode:** Customize text size and font style.
- **Highlights & Bookmarks:** Long-press any verse to highlight it in various colors or bookmark it.
- **Search:** A powerful search engine to find specific words or phrases across the entire Bible.
- **Reading Plans:** Subscribe to daily reading plans (e.g., "Read the Bible in a Year"). The app tracks progress automatically.

### 📝 Member Notes
- A personal digital notebook for sermons or personal study.
- **Action:** Tap "+ New Note", write a title, and type out notes. These are synced to their cloud account so they are never lost.

## 4. Media & Worship

### 🎧 Sermons
A library of all past messages preached at the church.
- **Tabs:** Browse by "All Sermons" or filter by specific Categories (e.g., *Sunday Service*, *Bible Study*).
- **Video Sermons:** Tapping a video sermon opens the built-in player (or YouTube app) to watch the message.
- **Audio Sermons:** Tapping an audio sermon opens the background audio player. Members can lock their phones and continue listening.
- **Notes Integration:** While listening, members can tap the "Take Notes" button to jot down thoughts directly linked to that sermon.

### 🎵 Song Book
A digital hymnal and worship lyric database.
- **Theme Songs:** The first tab highlights the Pastor's selected "Theme Songs" for the current month or season.
- **All Songs:** A searchable list of all church and global master songs.
- **Lyrics View:** Tapping a song displays the full lyrics (English & Telugu).
- **Lyrical Video:** If a YouTube link is attached, members can play the lyrical video at the top of the screen while reading the lyrics.

## 5. Community Engagement

### 📅 Events Calendar
- **List & Calendar View:** Members can toggle between a scrolling list of upcoming events or a visual month calendar.
- **Event Details:** Tapping an event shows the Date, Time, Venue, and Description.
- **RSVP:** If enabled by the Admin, members can tap the **"I'm Going"** button. If the event has a cap (e.g., 50 people), it shows how many spots are left.
- **Add to Calendar:** A button allows members to export the event directly to their phone's native Google/Apple Calendar.

### 📍 Pastor Itinerary Tracker
- If the Pastor is traveling and the event is public, members can see a "Live Route" map plotting the Pastor's expected arrival time at the venue.

### 💻 Online Meetings
- A dedicated screen for virtual Bible studies or services.
- **Upcoming:** Shows scheduled Zoom or Google Meet sessions.
- **Join:** When the session starts, the button turns green ("Join Now"). Tapping it seamlessly launches the meeting app on their phone.
- **Completed:** An archive of past online sessions.

## 6. Prayer Wall & Celebrations

### 🙏 The Prayer Wall
A social feed for congregational prayer.
- **Post a Request:** Members tap the "+" button to submit a personal prayer request. (Requests go to the Admin Dashboard for approval before appearing publicly).
- **Praying:** When viewing a request, members can tap the **"Praying 🙏"** button to let the person know they are being lifted up.
- **Comments:** Members can leave words of encouragement on a prayer request.
- **My Prayers:** A private tab showing the status (Pending/Approved) of their own submitted requests.

### 🎈 Celebrations
- **Upcoming Birthdays & Anniversaries:** A list of church members celebrating a milestone this week/month.
- **Live Chat / Wishes:** Tapping a celebrant opens a "Wishes Wall" where members can leave public birthday greetings for that person.

## 7. Giving & Subscriptions

### 💳 Giving (Tithes & Offerings)
A secure, integrated gateway for digital donations.
- **Select Fund:** Members choose where their money goes (e.g., *Tithes*, *Building Fund*, *Missions*).
- **Payment Methods:**
  - **Razorpay/Card:** Pay via Credit/Debit card or Net Banking.
  - **UPI Apps:** Direct deep-link to GPay, PhonePe, or Paytm (No typing required).
  - **Bank Transfer:** Displays the church's Account Name, Number, and IFSC for manual IMPS/NEFT transfers.
- **Instant Receipt:** Upon successful payment, the member instantly receives a digital receipt on-screen.

### 📜 Giving History
- Members can view a complete ledger of all their past donations.
- **Download PDF:** They can download formal PDF statements for their personal tax records.

### 🔄 Subscriptions (Recurring Giving)
- Members can set up auto-pay for monthly tithes or missionary pledges.
- They can manage, pause, or cancel their recurring subscriptions directly from this tab.

## 8. Church Information

### 🔔 Updates & Notifications
- Accessed via the Bell icon on the Home Screen.
- An inbox of all past Push Notifications, Emergency Broadcasts, and Announcements sent by the Admin.

### 👥 Church Directory
- A list of all active members in the church.
- **Privacy:** Members only see basic details (Name, Village). Sensitive data like exact DOB or Giving amounts are hidden.
- **Contact:** Members can tap to call or WhatsApp other members (if privacy settings allow).

### ℹ️ About Us & Contact Us
- **About:** Displays the Church's Mission, Vision, and history.
- **Contact:** Shows the physical address with a map preview. Tapping the address opens Google Maps for directions.
- **Socials:** Tappable links to the Church's YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp. Tapping the phone number opens the dialer, and tapping the email opens the mail app.
