HOW TO MANAGE, SCHEDULE & DESIGN DAILY PROMISES FROM THE ADMIN DASHBOARD
========================================================================

1. Starting the Process
-----------------------
From the main Admin Dashboard, look under the "Content Management" category:
- You will see the "Promises" card with a deep teal badge and holy book icon.
- Tapping "Promises" opens the Daily Promises Management Center (`AdminPromiseList.tsx`).
- In addition, you have two companion tabs:
  * "New Promise" (`AdminPromiseEditor.tsx`): Opens the full-featured promise creation and graphic design studio.
  * "Schedule" (`AdminPromiseCalendar.tsx`): Opens the 365-day calendar grid tracking missing and scheduled promises across the entire year.


=============================================================================================================================
2. PROMISE LIST SCREEN (AdminPromiseList.tsx) - ARCHIVE & TRACKING
=============================================================================================================================

When you open the Promises tab, you land on the Promises Dashboard:

A. Header Statistics Bar:
   - "Published": Total scriptures currently live and visible to church members.
   - "Draft": Promises saved as working drafts that are not yet visible to believers.
   - "Missing Dates": Live counter alerting the pastor how many upcoming calendar days this month are missing daily scripture promises!

B. Filter Pills:
   - "All": Displays complete promise archive in chronological order.
   - "Published": Filters exclusively live published scriptures.
   - "Draft": Shows only draft scriptures that need review before release.
   - "Missing": Filters calendar days with no assigned scripture, allowing the pastor to tap any missing day to schedule it immediately!

C. Today's Promise Hero Card:
   - Features a prominent gold border highlighting the active scripture for today.
   - Displays the Telugu Month & Day (e.g., "సెప్టెంబర్ 28"), the Telugu Verse, English Verse, and Reference.
   - Quick Action Buttons:
     * "Edit" (Pencil icon): Directly loads the promise into the design studio.
     * "Preview" (Eye icon): Shows how the scripture card looks to congregation members.

D. Upcoming & Past Promise Sections:
   - "Upcoming Promises": Lists upcoming scheduled scriptures awaiting their calendar date.
   - "Past Archives": Chronological history of past daily scriptures.


=============================================================================================================================
3. NEW PROMISE STUDIO (AdminPromiseEditor.tsx) - COMPLETE FIELD-BY-FIELD GUIDE
=============================================================================================================================

When you tap "+ New Promise" or edit an existing entry, the Promise Studio opens. It is organized into 9 clear sections:

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 1: LIVE THUMBNAIL VISUAL PREVIEW
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Located at the very top of the editor. As you type, pick colors, or select fonts, this card updates in REAL-TIME:
- Header Row: Church Logo + Church Name ("We Christian" or your church name) + Current Date.
- Center Scripture Text: Rendered in your selected Telugu font, size, weight, and color.
- Reference Badge: Book, Chapter, and Verse reference (e.g., "యెషయా 40:31").
- Footer: Pastor contact phone number and church blessing tagline.
- Action Buttons above the Preview:
  * "Share Poster" (Send icon): Captures the live card as a high-resolution JPEG flyer and opens WhatsApp to share!
  * "Save to Gallery": Downloads the generated image directly to your phone's photo gallery.


-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 2: CALENDAR DATE PICKER
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- `Promise Date` (Required):
  * Tap the calendar field to open the full Month & Year Navigator.
  * You can schedule promises days, weeks, or months in advance.
  * The selected date automatically calculates and displays the proper Telugu date header (e.g., "అక్టోబర్ 05").


-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 3: BUILT-IN TELUGU BIBLE SELECTOR (ONE-TAP SCRIPTURE IMPORT)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Instead of typing long Telugu Bible verses manually, the editor includes a built-in offline Telugu Bible:
- Step 1: Tap "Select Book" -> Dropdown opens with all 66 Old & New Testament Telugu book names (e.g., 'ఆదికాండము', 'కీర్తనలు', 'యోహాను సువార్త').
- Step 2: Tap "Select Chapter" -> Number selector for all chapters in that book.
- Step 3: Tap "Select Verse" -> Number selector for the exact verse.
- Result: The app INSTANTLY pulls the authentic Telugu scripture text and fills both the Telugu Verse and Reference fields automatically!


-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 4: AI PROMISE GENERATOR (WAND / SPARKLES TOOL)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For busy pastors preparing messages:
- Tap the "AI Scripture Assistant" (Sparkles icon).
- Enter any spiritual theme or pastoral topic (e.g., "Peace in Anxiety", "Healing", "Financial Breakthrough", "New Year Blessing").
- Tap "Generate Promise":
  * The AI searches the Bible for the most powerful promise matching that topic.
  * Automatically populates the English reference, English verse, Telugu reference, Telugu verse, and a short pastoral reflection!


-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 5: SCRIPTURE & DEVOTIONAL TEXT INPUT FIELDS
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Telugu Scripture Fields (Primary for South Indian Congregations):
   - `Telugu Reference` (Required): e.g., "యిర్మియా 29:11" or "కీర్తనలు 23:1".
   - `Telugu Verse Text` (Required): The full promise text in Telugu script.
   - `Telugu Devotional Reflection (Optional)`: 2-3 sentences of pastoral encouragement or practical application.

B. English Scripture Fields:
   - `English Reference` (Required): e.g., "Jeremiah 29:11" or "Psalm 23:1".
   - `English Verse Text` (Required): The full promise text in English (KJV / NIV / ESV).
   - `English Devotional Note (Optional)`: English reflection for bilingual church members and youth.


-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 6: TYPOGRAPHY & TELUGU FONT STUDIO
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Customize the exact look and feel of the Telugu text on the promise poster:
- `Font Family Selector`: Choose between 11 authentic Telugu typography fonts:
  * Suranna (Classic, elegant serif - Recommended)
  * Gidugu (Modern, clean)
  * Gurajada (Traditional poetic font)
  * Mallanna, Mandali, NTR, Peddana, Ramabhadra, Ramaraja, TenaliRamakrishna, Timmana
- `Font Size Stepper`: Increase (+) or decrease (-) text size (Presets: 9.0 to 18.0) to fit long or short verses perfectly.
- `Font Weight`: Toggle between Normal (400), Semi-Bold (600), Bold (700), or Extra Bold (800).
- `Italics`: Toggle italic styling on/off.
- `Text Alignment`: Align Left, Center, or Right.
- `Text Vertical Position`: Place text at the Top, Center, or Bottom of the poster.
- `Text Color Palette`: Pick from curated text colors (Pure White, Warm Gold, Sunburst Yellow, Soft Cyan, Pearl Pink) or enter a custom Hex code.


-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 7: BACKGROUND ARTWORK & COLOR PALETTES
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Choose what appears behind the scripture text:
A. Mode 1: Curated Spiritual Wallpapers (Default):
   - Browse the built-in library of high-resolution Christian themes (Sunrises, Open Bibles, Cross on the Hill, Peaceful Waters, Praying Hands).
   - Tap any wallpaper to apply it instantly to the preview.

B. Mode 2: Custom Photo Upload:
   - Tap "Upload Custom Image" to pick a photo from your phone gallery (e.g., church altar, choir, Sunday service photo).
   - Includes automatic brightness darkening so white text remains 100% legible over any photo!

C. Mode 3: Solid Color Themes:
   - Select rich, deep colors: Royal Navy, Deep Emerald, Imperial Purple, Rich Crimson, Dark Chocolate, Midnight Slate.

D. Mode 4: Dual-Tone Gradients:
   - Choose smooth modern gradients: Ocean Blue to Purple, Emerald to Teal, Sunset Coral to Ruby.


-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 8: AUDIO BLESSING & VIDEO DEVOTIONAL INTEGRATION
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Enhance the scripture beyond static text with multimedia:
- `YouTube Devotional URL (Optional)`:
  * Paste a YouTube video or Shorts link of the pastor sharing a 2-minute morning message.
  * The app automatically extracts the video ID and embeds a playable video right under the promise!
- `Video Title`: e.g., "Morning Manna with Pastor Daniel".
- `Preacher / Pastor Name`: Identifies the speaker.
- `Duration`: e.g., "3:45 Min".


-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 9: PUBLICATION STATUS & SAVING
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Choose how and when the promise goes live:
- `Draft — Save only, not visible`: Saves your progress. Visible only to admins for editing.
- `Scheduled — Auto-publish at midnight`: Default setting. The app automatically publishes the scripture at 12:00 AM on that calendar date!
- `Publish now — Live immediately`: Instantly pushes the scripture to all members' home screens immediately.

Action Buttons:
- "Save Promise" (Primary Green Button): Validates fields and saves to Firestore cloud database.
- "Cancel / Back": Safely returns to the promises list.


=============================================================================================================================
4. SCHEDULE CALENDAR VIEW (AdminPromiseCalendar.tsx)
=============================================================================================================================

Tapping the "Schedule" card in the Admin Dashboard opens the 365-Day Annual Promise Calendar:
- Monthly Grid: Displays every day of the month with quick status badges.
- Visual Color Coding:
  * Green Box: The date has an active scripture scheduled and ready to publish.
  * Yellow Box: The date has a draft saved but not yet finalized.
  * Red Box / Warning: Missing scripture! Alerts pastors that congregation members have no promise assigned for that morning.
- Quick Jump: Tap any date on the calendar to instantly open the New Promise editor pre-set to that date!


=============================================================================================================================
5. HOW CONGREGATION MEMBERS EXPERIENCE PROMISES IN THE APP
=============================================================================================================================

When a church believer opens the WeChristian mobile app:
1. First Thing They See (Home Screen Hero):
   - Today's Daily Promise appears right at the top of the home screen as a holy devotional greeting.
2. Telugu & English Switching:
   - A single-tap language toggle lets members switch between Telugu script and English text instantly.
3. Audio / Video Playback:
   - If the pastor attached a YouTube video or voice blessing, members see a "Watch Morning Message" button that plays smoothly inside the app.
4. WhatsApp Sharing:
   - Members can tap the WhatsApp Share icon on the card to forward the spiritual promise card to their family, friends, and church prayer groups with church branding attached!
5. Daily Archive:
   - Members can tap "Previous Promises" to browse past days and meditate on God's Word throughout the week.
