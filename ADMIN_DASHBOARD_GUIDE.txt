1. OVERVIEW & ACCESS LEVELS (CHURCH ADMIN VS. SUPER ADMIN)
=============================================================================================================================

WeChristian operates on two distinct administrative tiers:

A. Church Admin / Pastor (`userType = "ADMIN" | "PASTOR"`):
   - Scope: Restricted exclusively to their own church congregation and its satellite branches.
   - Purpose: Day-to-day pastoral duties, Sunday services, member communication, attendance, local finances, events.

B. Platform Super Admin (`isPlatformSuperAdmin = true`):
   - Scope: Platform-wide governance over all churches registered in the WeChristian ecosystem.
   - Purpose: Manage church subscriptions, create voucher passes, publish master songs, curate global scriptures.


=============================================================================================================================
2. DASHBOARD NAVIGATION & HERO BANNER
=============================================================================================================================

When an admin logs in, they land on the main Admin Dashboard (`AdminDashboard.tsx`):

A. Hero Banner Section:
   - Church Logo: Displays the active church's branding circular badge.
   - Church Name: Bold display of the church congregation.
   - Subtitle: Dynamic greeting ("Good morning / afternoon / evening, Pastor [Name]").
   - Impersonation Pill (Multi-Branch Churches): If viewing a branch campus, a top indicator appears:
     "Viewing Branch: [Branch Name] | [Exit Button]". Tapping Exit safely returns to the main headquarters.

B. Module Cards Grid:
   - Features rich visual cards organized into 9 color-coded thematic categories.
   - Full-width hero cards for high-frequency modules (Songs, Members, Subscription, WeCelebrations).

3. CATEGORY 1: CONTENT MANAGEMENT
=============================================================================================================================

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 PROMISES (DAILY PROMISES LIST - AdminPromiseList.tsx)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Manages the daily scripture promises pushed to congregation member home screens every morning and evening.

- Search & Filters:
  * Search Bar: Filter scriptures by keyword, book name, or Telugu text.
  * Date Filter: Jump to any specific day of the year.
- Promise Card Elements:
  * Scripture Reference: Book, Chapter, Verse (e.g., "Isaiah 40:31" / "యెషయా 40:31").
  * Scripture Text: Telugu and English scripture promise.
  * Background Theme: Spiritual aesthetic backdrop selected for that date.
  * Status Badge: "Scheduled" vs "Published".
- Card Actions:
  * Edit (Pencil): Opens the promise in the editor.
  * Preview (Eye): Simulates how members view it on their phone home screen.
  * Delete (Trash): Removes the scripture entry.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.2 NEW PROMISE (PROMISE EDITOR - AdminPromiseEditor.tsx)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Form used to draft, design, and schedule a daily scripture.

- Input Fields:
  * `Schedule Date` (Date Picker): Choose calendar day for publication.
  * `Time Slot` (Dropdown): Morning Slot (6:00 AM) or Evening Slot (6:00 PM).
  * `Scripture Reference (English)`: e.g., "Jeremiah 29:11".
  * `Verse Text (English)`: English scripture text.
  * `Scripture Reference (Telugu)`: e.g., "యిర్మియా 29:11".
  * `Verse Text (Telugu)`: Telugu script scripture text.
  * `Background Artwork Picker`: Select from curated spiritual photo library or upload custom church photo.
  * `Audio Blessing URL (Optional)`: Link to pastor's short audio voice prayer for the day.
  * `Publish Immediately Toggle`: Push live now vs keep scheduled.
- Action Buttons:
  * "Save Promise": Validates Telugu & English fields and syncs to Firestore.
  * "Discard": Cancels edits.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.3 SCHEDULE (PROMISE CALENDAR - AdminPromiseCalendar.tsx)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A full 365-day calendar grid mapping daily promises.
- Green Dots: Dates with both Morning and Evening promises completed.
- Yellow Dots: Dates with only one slot filled.
- Red Dots: Upcoming dates missing scripture promises (alerts pastor to schedule).
- Tapping any date instantly opens that day's editor.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.4 SERMONS (SERMON ARCHIVE - AdminSermonList.tsx)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Central repository of church video recordings, livestreams, and podcast messages.

- Header Metrics: Total Sermons Published, Total Views, Featured Sermons.
- Sermon Card Elements:
  * Video Thumbnail: Auto-fetched from YouTube link or custom church artwork.
  * Sermon Title: Topic preached (e.g., "Walking in Divine Favor").
  * Preacher / Speaker Name: Lead pastor, guest speaker, or youth minister.
  * Service Date: Date sermon was delivered.
  * Category / Series: e.g., "Faith Series", "Sunday Morning", "Youth Fest".
  * Platform Badges: YouTube, MP3 Audio, or PDF Notes available.
- Actions:
  * Edit: Updates sermon details or video URL.
  * Feature on App Home: Toggles sermon to member app top carousel.
  * Delete: Removes sermon from archive.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.5 NEW SERMON (SERMON EDITOR - AdminSermonEditor.tsx)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Form for publishing Sunday sermons and teachings.

- Input Fields:
  * `Sermon Title` (Required): Catchy, inspirational title.
  * `Preacher Name` (Required): Speaker's full title & name.
  * `Sermon Date` (Date Picker): Date service took place.
  * `Service Type` (Dropdown): Sunday 1st Service, Sunday 2nd Service, Midweek, Youth, Fasting Prayer.
  * `Bible Passage Reference`: Primary scriptures (e.g., "Romans 8:28-39").
  * `YouTube / Video Link`: Standard YouTube link (e.g., https://youtu.be/...). Auto-extracts thumbnail and video ID.
  * `Audio Podcast URL (Optional)`: Direct MP3 link for audio listeners.
  * `Sermon Outline / Notes`: Pastor's message notes, 3 key points, and application questions.
  * `Featured Switch`: Highlights on the church app landing screen.
- Action: "Publish Sermon".

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.6 SONGS (HYMNAL & LYRICS EDITOR - AdminSongEditor.tsx)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The local church worship hymnal. Supplements the platform's global master song library.

- Filters:
  * Alphabet Ribbon (A-Z + All): Quick indexed search.
  * Category Selector: Stuthi (Praise), Aradhana (Worship), Offering, Christmas, Easter, Youth, Gospel, Wedding.
- Song Card Details:
  * Song Number, English Title, Telugu Script Title, Category Tag, Chord Sheet availability.
- Add / Edit Song Modal Fields:
  * `Title (English)`: Romanized song name.
  * `Title (Telugu)`: Pure Telugu script title.
  * `Category`: Multi-check tags.
  * `Scale / Key (Optional)`: Musical scale (e.g., "D Major", "G Minor").
  * `Tempo / BPM`: Beat tempo (e.g., "85 BPM").
  * `Song Lyrics`: Complete lyrics with Stanza 1, Chorus, Stanza 2 formatting.
  * `YouTube Demo URL`: Link to worship reference song.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
12.2 VOUCHERS TAB (SuperAdminVouchersManager.tsx)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Generates, prints, and tracks 1-Year Church Subscription Voucher Passes.

- Code Format (Captcha Style):
  * Exactly 7 Characters alphanumeric (e.g. `7K8N2XP`, `4M9W3QA`, `8B2P9XM`).
  * Guaranteed mix of capital letters and numbers.
  * Uses unambiguous characters (excludes confusing 0/O and 1/I).
- Single Voucher Modal:
  * `Voucher Code`: Auto-generate 7-character captcha code or type custom.
  * `Duration (Days)`: Default 365 (1 year access).
  * `Voucher Validity (Days)`: Redemption window before code expires.
  * `Batch Tag`: Optional label.
- Bulk Voucher Modal:
  * `Number of Vouchers (1 - 100)`: Batch generate up to 100 unique 7-digit captcha codes.
  * `Duration Days`: Default 365 days.
  * `Validity Days`: Claim deadline.
  * `Batch Tag`: Group name (e.g., "Pastors Conference 2026").
- Utility Bar:
  * `Print Cards (PDF)`: Renders printable gold passes with QR code, Play Store URL, and 7-character code.
  * `Export CSV`: Downloads complete voucher spreadsheet file.
- Filter Pills (with live counts):
  * `All (N)`, `Unused (N)`, `Redeemed (N)`, `Expired (N)`, `Disabled (N)`.
- Card Actions:
  * Copy Code, Share to WhatsApp, Disable/Enable Toggle, Delete (with custom warning and success modals).

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
12.3 MASTER SONGS TAB
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Global hymn library automatically shared with all churches.
- Alphabet Ribbon (A-Z + All) & Bilingual Search (Telugu & English).
- Add Master Song: Title (English), Title (Telugu), Categories, Lyrics.
- Bulk Upload: Ingest 500+ hymns at once via CSV / JSON file.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
12.4 VERSES TAB (SuperAdminVersesManager.tsx)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Global 365-day promise engine powering member home screens.
- Month selector (Jan - Dec) and 4 Daily Periods (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Night).
- Fields: English Scripture & Reference, Telugu Scripture & Reference.
