1. MODULE OVERVIEW & STEWARDSHIP ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================
The Donations module (`DonationsDashboard.tsx`) is a secure, double-authenticated 
church financial stewardship platform. It enables church leaders to record, verify, 
audit, and acknowledge tithes, love offerings, mission pledges, and building fund 
contributions.

Core Capabilities:
* Complete Stewardship Tracking: Track Sunday collections, personal tithes, building 
  fund contributions, and designated mission offerings.
* Member Autocomplete: Rapidly lookup registered church members to record offerings 
  without repetitive manual data entry.
* Online Donation Verification: Review and approve digital offerings submitted by 
  congregation members through the WeChristian mobile app.
* Official Receipts & Tax Certificates: Generate instant branded receipts formatted for 
  tax exemptions (such as Section 80G in India) with automated sequential numbering.
* Direct WhatsApp Delivery: Deliver official receipts to donors' WhatsApp chats within 
  seconds of receiving their gift.

================================================================================
Located in the Admin Dashboard under "Church Ledger & Finances" as "Donations":

================================================================================
3. TIME-PERIOD FILTERING & AUDITING VIEWS
================================================================================
Provides 6 flexible time filters at the top of the screen:
1. "Today": Offerings received on the current calendar date (ideal for Sunday count).
2. "Week": Offerings received across the rolling 7-day period.
3. "Month": Total monthly collections.
4. "Year": Year-to-date giving for the current calendar year.
5. "Custom Range": Opens dual modal date pickers (`DateTimePickerModal`) for specific 
   audit dates (e.g., Christmas convention week).
6. "All Time": Cumulative historical donations record since church onboarding.

4. DONATION DASHBOARD VIEW (SubTab: 'dashboard')
================================================================================
4.1 Total Collections Summary Banner
------------------------------------
* Deep royal navy card (`#1b2a4a`) with gold accents (`#c9973f`).
* Displays:
  - Active Filter Badge (e.g., "THIS MONTH", "TODAY", "YEAR 2026").
  - Grand Total Giving: Prominently formatted in Indian Rupees (e.g., `₹3,85,000`).
  - Active church branch name.

4.2 Category Collections Breakdown
----------------------------------
Four pre-configured primary stewardship funds:
1. "Tithes" (దశమభాగములు): Personal monthly tithes from church members.
2. "Offering" (కానుకలు): Sunday morning general offerings, worship offerings.
3. "Building Fund" (భవన నిధి): Capital campaign giving, land acquisition, sanctuary repair.
4. "Missionary" (మిషనరీ పరిచర్య): Support for rural evangelists, church planters, outreach.
5. Custom Funds: Additional categories configured by the church (e.g., "Youth Camp", "Charity").
Each card displays the count of donors and the total rupee amount collected.

4.3 Online Pending Donations Alert Card
---------------------------------------
* Appears dynamically whenever members have submitted online offerings requiring approval.
* Displays: "X Online Donations Pending Review".
* Tapping the card opens the Pending Donations Review Modal.

4.4 Recent Collections Activity Stream
--------------------------------------
A chronological list of the most recent donations displaying:
* Donor full name and phone number.
* Category badge (e.g., `Tithes` / `Building Fund`).
* Payment method tag (e.g., `Cash`, `Online / UPI`, `Bank Transfer`).
* Rupee amount.

5. DONATIONS LEDGER & DIRECTORY (SubTab: 'donations')
================================================================================
The master donor database providing comprehensive search and audit records.

5.1 Real-Time Donor Search & Keyword Matching
---------------------------------------------
* Search bar dynamically filters the ledger in real time by:
  - Donor Name (First name, middle name, or surname)
  - Donor Phone Number
  - Receipt Number (e.g., "RCPT-WEC-0000045")
  - Specific Notes content

5.2 Category Filter Modal
-------------------------
* Allows filtering the ledger table to isolate a single fund (e.g., viewing only 
  "Building Fund" contributors for a capital project report).

5.3 Donation Card Anatomy
-------------------------
Each recorded donation is rendered with full financial clarity:
* Category Badge: Top-left tag with gold styling.
* Donor Name & Phone: e.g., "Bro. K. Daniel • +91 98480 12345".
* Receipt Number: e.g., "Receipt: RCPT-WEC-0000045".
* Donation Date: Formatted date (e.g., "14 Oct 2026").
* Payment Method Tag: Green `Cash` badge or Indigo `Online / UPI` badge.
* Total Amount: Bold rupee figure (e.g., `₹10,000`).
* Action Buttons:
  - Receipt Icon (FileText): Instantly launches the official printable receipt.
  - Pencil Icon: Opens the edit modal to update amounts or notes.
  - Trash Icon: Prompts destructive confirmation to delete record.

================================================================================
6. RECORDING A NEW DONATION (Add Donation Modal)
================================================================================
Accessible via the `+ Record Donation` button in the top bar.

6.1 Intelligent Church Member Autocomplete
------------------------------------------
* As the administrator begins typing in the "Donor Name" field, an autocomplete dropdown 
  suggests registered church members matching the text.
* Tapping a suggested member automatically populates their full legal name and contact phone.
* Non-members / Guest donors can also be typed manually.

6.2 Donor Phone Number Auto-Fill
--------------------------------
* Automatically binds the donor's mobile phone number for instant WhatsApp receipt delivery.

6.3 Category Selection & Custom Category Creator
------------------------------------------------
* Category Dropdown: Tithes, Offering, Building Fund, Missionary, Other.
* "+ Add Custom Category": Allows the treasurer to create new funds on the fly 
  (e.g., "Medical Aid Fund", "Orphanage Support").
* Automatically persists new categories to the church's Firestore document.

6.4 Amount Input & Currency Formatting
--------------------------------------
* Numeric input with currency symbol (`₹`). Validates that amounts are greater than zero.

6.5 Payment Method & Date
-------------------------
* Payment Method: Cash, Online / UPI, Cheque, Bank Transfer.
* Date Picker: Calendar modal defaulting to today.
* Notes: Optional text input for special dedication intentions, memorial gifts, or 
  family thanksgiving prayers.

6.6 Instant Post-Save Receipt Launch
------------------------------------
* Upon tapping "Save Donation", the record is committed to Firestore, an animated 
  success notification appears, and the app instantly launches the official receipt 
  preview for printing or WhatsApp delivery!

7. ONLINE DONATIONS VERIFICATION & APPROVAL WORKFLOW
================================================================================
When members contribute online through the WeChristian member portal:

7.1 Pending Member Donations Review Modal
-----------------------------------------
* Accessible from the dashboard alert card.
* Displays a list of all unverified digital offerings.

7.2 Verification of Online Transaction Proof
--------------------------------------------
* Shows:
  - Donor Name and registered phone number.
  - Designated category (e.g., Tithes).
  - Claimed amount.
  - Digital Transaction ID / UPI Reference Number / Bank UTR.
  - Uploaded screenshot of the UPI payment receipt (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm).

7.3 One-Tap Approval & Rejection Actions
----------------------------------------
* "Approve Donation" Button:
  - Verifies the funds have cleared the church's bank account.
  - Updates donation status to `Success`.
  - Automatically incorporates the sum into the official church ledger.
  - Dispatches an automated confirmation receipt to the member's app.
* "Reject Donation" Button:
  - Flags the submission as `Rejected` with a customizable rejection reason.

8. OFFICIAL DONATION RECEIPTS & 80G TAX INVOICES
================================================================================
8.1 Sequential Receipt Numbering System
---------------------------------------
* Automatically issues tamper-evident sequential receipt codes:
  `RCPT-[CHURCH_CODE]-[SEQUENCE_NUMBER]`
  (e.g., `RCPT-WEC-0000045` or `RCPT-CAL-0000108`).

8.2 Single Donation Receipt Generation
--------------------------------------
* Generates an official, legal-grade digital receipt for individual givers.

8.3 Category / Period Batch Financial Summary Report
----------------------------------------------------
* Tapping the Report icon in the top header allows treasurers to generate a consolidated 
  batch report of all donations for a specific category or time frame (e.g., a complete 
  monthly tithes report for the church finance committee).

8.4 Legal & Tax-Compliant Receipt Template (HTML5 Layout)
---------------------------------------------------------
The rendered template includes:
* Church Branding Header: High-res Church Crest/Logo, Church Legal Name, Registered 
  Address, and Phone.
* Metadata Block: Receipt Number, Donation Date, Received From (Donor Name), and 
  Donor Phone.
* Line Items Table: Columns for `DONATION CATEGORY`, `PAYMENT METHOD`, and `AMOUNT`.
* Notes / Dedication: Displays donor's special prayer intention or memorial note.
* Grand Total Box: High-contrast navy container with prominent rupee amount.
* Pastoral Blessing:
  *"Thank you for your generous donation. May God bless you abundantly."*
* Formal Audit Sign-off: Dedicated dotted boxes for "Church Seal" and "Authorized Signature".

================================================================================
9. PRINTING, EXPORT & DONOR WHATSAPP DISPATCH
================================================================================
9.1 PDF Compilation & Wireless AirPrint (expo-print)
----------------------------------------------------
* "Print PDF" compiles the receipt into an official vector PDF.
* Connects directly to office wireless printers for physical paper receipt issuing.

9.2 High-Resolution Graphic Snapshot (ViewShot Engine)
------------------------------------------------------
* `ViewShot.capture()` renders the entire receipt off-screen at full native pixel 
  resolution into a crystal-clear PNG image.

9.3 Save Receipt to Mobile Gallery (MediaLibrary Integration)
-------------------------------------------------------------
* Saves a digital copy of the receipt image directly to the phone's native Photos library 
  under the "WeChristian" album.

9.4 Direct WhatsApp Dispatch to Donor's Phone
---------------------------------------------
* Tapping "Share Receipt":
  - Launches native sharing (`Sharing.shareAsync`).
  - Pre-selects or opens WhatsApp to send the receipt image and message directly to 
    the donor's phone number.

10. DATABASE SCHEMA & FIRESTORE INTEGRATION
================================================================================
Donation records are stored in the Firestore collection `church_donations`:

Field               | Type             | Description
--------------------+------------------+------------------------------------------------
id                  | string           | Unique donation document auto-ID
churchId            | string           | Associated church document ID
category            | string           | 'Tithes', 'Offering', 'Building Fund', etc.
donorName           | string           | Member or guest donor name
donorPhone          | string           | Contact telephone number
amount              | number           | Contribution in INR (₹)
date                | string (YYYY-MM-DD)| Transaction date
paymentMethod       | string           | 'Cash', 'Online', 'UPI', 'Cheque', 'Bank Transfer'
status              | string           | 'Success' | 'Pending' | 'Rejected'
notes               | string           | Dedication, prayer request, or UTR number
receiptNo           | string           | Formatted receipt number (e.g., 'RCPT-WEC-0000045')
addedBy             | string           | Administrator name or member ID
createdAt           | timestamp        | Server creation timestamp

11. CHURCH STEWARDSHIP & FINANCIAL BEST PRACTICES
================================================================================
1. Immediate Receipt Issuance:
   - Always issue a digital WhatsApp receipt immediately after counting Sunday offerings. 
     This builds deep trust and financial transparency across the congregation.
2. Dual Custody Counting:
   - Ensure two church council members or deacons are present when counting physical cash 
     offerings and recording them into the Donations ledger.
3. Annual Giving Statements:
   - At the close of the financial year (March 31 in India), use the Category / Period 
     batch report to issue annual giving tax statements for income tax deductions.
